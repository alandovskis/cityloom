# Introduction

CityLoom is a street, junction and city-map editor. It is written in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly and drawn with
[Leptos](https://leptos.dev/) 0.8 (client-side rendering). It runs in the browser; it has no back end, and a static file server serves its files.

The streets come from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/). The rules, rates and thresholds of the
catalogue (what a check measures a street against) are synthetic placeholders, not a standard.

The app is available in English and Canadian French.

## The four pages

| Page     | File                    | What it is for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home     | `web/index.html`        | Find a place: search for a city or a neighbourhood and load its streets.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Map      | `web/map.html`          | The city's streets and junctions over a basemap. Open a place to edit it, and declare areas with rules (the Area rules panel). The Network checks, under the Checks tab of the Notes panel, look across the whole city: cycling links cut off from the rest, bike and transit lanes that stop at a junction, pedestrian streets without an unbroken sidewalk, arms without a crossing or with too wide a one, lanes that jump in number where two streets join, and walking routes (a sidewalk that stops, a part of the city people cannot walk to from the rest, and a block longer than 250 m). They are counted apart from each place's own checks, and each finding links the places it concerns. The Provenance tab of the Notes panel shows where the OpenStreetMap data behind the map may be missing or wrong (a street with no name, streets joined to nothing, dead ends, implausible widths, no speed limit), each with its source and what it could mislead; these are not design failures and are counted apart from every check. The curbside uses of a street are drawn beside it from zoom 16 and its transit stops from zoom 15, and pressing one opens the street. |
| Street   | `web/street.html`       | Edit one street, drawn in 2.5D: arrange its pieces, read the checks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Junction | `web/intersection.html` | Edit one junction in plan: its arms, corners, crossings and how it is controlled. The curbside uses and transit stops of a street are drawn on the arm that meets it, to 36 m from the centre, and the Checks fail parking or loading that stands within 5 m of a crossing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

The street and junction pages are opened from the map: the map gives each place an address such as `?street=7` or
`?junction=3`. A page opened that way edits one place of the city, and its edits are kept in the browser's storage.
