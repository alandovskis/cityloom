# Surface brief: home page

Scope: one surface, `web/index.html`, the site root. Mode: Persuade (an entry page for an Operate product). Code-led. It inherits the established world recorded in `web-map-html`; the street editor that used to live at this address is now `web/street.html` and has its own brief.

Audience and job: a resident arriving for the first time or returning. Within seconds they understand what CityLoom is for, can search for a street or junction (the main action), open the whole-city map, or start a new street, and see how the city stands.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One floating card over the real sample city: the headline, the big search, and the way in. The city behind it is the proof. It refuses a generic white-card-on-grey SaaS landing and any invented claim.

OWN-WORLD: The map's tokens; a 16px card, a white search field with a clear 2px edge and a filled blue Search button, system type with one large headline, the city drawn as the map draws it, eased in from the card's edge and running off the right and bottom. Sample data is labelled as sample.

STORY: The resident reads one line, types a street name, and lands in its editor; or opens the map; or starts a blank street. If a place needs attention, the line under the buttons says so in amber.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Bar (logo, Map/Street/Intersection, avatar). Card at the left: headline, one-line lead, search with Search button, "Open the city map" and "Start a new street", how the city stands, the sample note. The city to its right, bleeding off the edge. The card uses the system radii (16px card, 14px search, 12px buttons).

FORM: The category standard (user's choice), benchmark Streetmix.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
