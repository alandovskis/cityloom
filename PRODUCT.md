# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Rust compiled to WebAssembly for the web MVP (user's choice). Native Android and iOS apps are planned to follow the web release; the stack should favor sharing core logic across platforms. Deploy target: undecided.

## Users

Residents and citizens of a city. Their job is to compose and explore whole-city scenarios: how the city could be laid out or governed differently, and what that would do.

## Product Purpose

CityLoom is "Streetmix at city scale". Streetmix lets people design a single street cross-section; CityLoom lifts that to whole-city scenarios that residents can compose and compare. Success means ordinary residents can build a city-wide scenario and understand its outcomes without professional planning tools.

## Positioning

A resident-facing, open tool for composing whole-city scenarios, in the way Streetmix made street design approachable for non-experts.

## Operating Context

Used by non-professional residents, first in a web browser, later in native mobile apps. Built on open-source code and open data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Web MVP first; Android and iOS follow.
- Rust and WebAssembly stack.
- Open-source and open data are binding constraints.
- Undecided: which city or cities are supported, the specific data sources, the scenario model (which levers and outcomes exist), how scenarios are saved or shared, licensing, and deploy target.

## Brand Commitments

The name CityLoom only. No logo, voice, or identity assets exist yet.

## Evidence on Hand

None. The repository is empty. Do not fabricate data, outcomes, users, testimonials, or benchmarks.

## Product Principles

- Approachable for non-experts: a resident should be able to act without planning training.
- Whole-city scale: scenarios describe the city, not one street.
- Open by default: open-source code and open data.
- One core, many surfaces: web first, with logic reusable by later native apps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

An accessibility standard is required for public civic use. The specific level was not stated. WCAG 2.2 AA is assumed and should be confirmed.
