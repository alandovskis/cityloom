# City, import and persistence (`src/city/`)

Loaded when working in this folder; the root `CLAUDE.md` has the project-wide rules.

## The slice
- `keys.rs` (place keys), `edits.rs` (edits document), `model.rs`, `store.rs` (storage), `projection.rs` (`Projection`: where a kept city lies on the earth), `binding.rs` (`CityBinding`, what a page writes back through), `import.rs`.
- `Layout::sample()` is the tests' hand-made city, not part of the app; likewise `street::model::fixtures` and `junction::model::fixtures` (test-only).
- An edge keeps the road's centreline (`EdgeDef.shape`, `EdgeView.shape_mm`, layout mm); the layout remembers where it sits on the earth (`Layout.origin_m`, `CityView.origin_m`) for the map. Only the editors and the home hero draw an edge as a straight line.

## Import from OpenStreetMap
- `cityloom_model` is the plain network type (serde only): a `Network` of `Junction`s (position, provenance, control) and `Road`s (an `Edge` with its id and nodes, provenance, name, `RoadType`, `Zone`s, centre line), `Node`/`Edge` the bare graph derived from it, `Corridor`s by user class. It is kept as its types are written (no wire structs), so a change to a type is a decision about `IMPORTER_VERSION` (in `cityloom_model`), which makes kept areas be read again. `cityloom_model` also holds `Frame` and `NetworkTile` (a network with the frame it is drawn in) and `Network::{reaching, absorb, clip_to}`. The importer, `citymodel_builder::importer` (feature `import`), runs osm2streets (git dependencies) and fills it; it runs only in the builder, never in the browser: the editor reads finished JSON network tiles.
- `import.rs` (`Layout::from_network`) turns a network into a `Layout`: roads become streets (`Street::imported`, with their `name` and a `StreetClass` from the road's `RoadType`; a road with no lanes is left out; a place's title derives from the streets that meet there). A meeting of 3-5 arms the junction editor can draw becomes a junction (arms within 30 degrees are spread); otherwise a plain connection.
- A place is flagged only for checks a change makes worse than the city first laid out.

### Buffers and widths
- A lane buffer keeps osm2streets' type (`cityloom_model::BufferType`, `Lane.buffer_type`; sidepath zipping is not among the transformations the importer runs). `kind_of` makes it planting only for a planter or a verge; a curb, paint, posts, a barrier, a shoulder (the catalogue's `shoulder` is a motor kind; osm2streets gives that name to any lane open to every mode) or an unknown type is the neutral `buffer` kind (last of `KINDS`, so stored indices hold; concrete or asphalt, mode `Neutral`, in no mode's share of the width, not `is_roadway`, no line painted against it).
- A curb between a bike lane or sidewalk and a driving, parking or transit lane belongs to that piece: its width is added to the piece's (up to its maximum). What passes the maximum stays beside it as a buffer; a remainder under the buffer's 150 mm minimum goes to the road lane beside the curb. The street keeps its width either way. A median is only the space between two merged carriageways.
- Legacy widths (live compatibility): merging curbs changed `row_mm` for most streets (importer version 5), and a saved edit applies only when `row_mm` matches. The importer therefore also computes `EdgeDef.legacy_rows_mm` (never saved, empty where none differs): the widths under importer version 4 (every buffer its own median, nothing merged), with a two-way bike lane read as a one-way lane (half width), and both. A record whose `row_mm` is the current width or a legacy one applies (current tried first); a legacy-width record is shown as saved, keeps that width on every write and is found by it at each import; Start over gives the current width; a record matching none stays an orphan. `city::store` pins this; `real_city` checks all 150 changed Plateau streets are found. `city::edits::VERSION` is unaffected.
- Speed: from `maxspeed` (`cityloom_model::Zone.speed_kmh` of the first zone, `Road::base_speed_kmh()`, whole km/h; missing or unreadable gives none; no default is assumed anywhere). Two merged carriageways take the lower known speed, else the known one, else none. `from_network` sets `Speed::Kmh` or `Speed::NotSet` (`Street::imported` leaves `Undecided`).

### Bike tracks
- A two-way track is one bike lane with no direction (`cityloom_model::Direction::Both`, the editor's `direction: None`). the importer reads it from `cycleway:<side>:oneway=no`; osm2streets keeps neither separately mapped tracks nor a lane's two directions.
- For `cycleway:<side>=separate`, before osm2streets runs the importer ties the road's side to the tracks that belong to it (`highway=cycleway`, or `path`/`footway` with `bicycle=designated`), then joins the two opposite lanes osm2streets makes of a `oneway=no` side.
  - A track belongs to one carriageway (motorway to unclassified, residential, living_street, busway, `service` other than driveway, parking_aisle, drive-through, alley) among those it runs along (60% of five points along it within the road's extent, 1.5 to 16 m from its centre line on a side, within 30 degrees): the road saying `separate` on that side first, then the nearest, then the lower way id.
  - The side is two-way when no such track is one-way (`oneway:bicycle` / `bicycle:oneway` before `oneway`). A track 5 to 16 m from a tagged street that really follows a railway or river is claimed all the same; each piece a clip cuts is judged alone.
- Tracks come with the roads: `citymodel_builder`'s `tiles` keeps them (cycleways, plus paths/footways designated for bicycles from a second `osmium tags-filter` pass, since one expression cannot AND two tags) for the importer. The importer leaves every cycleway, path and footway out of what it hands osm2streets, so they are neither streets nor junctions.

### Where a place's roads come from (`place/loader.rs`)
- From the metro network tiles (`data/metro/<x>_<y>.json`, a `NetworkTile`; `place/tiles.rs`, `data/metro/index.json` names the grid and tiles): the tile whose core holds the place's centre, plus any other listed tile the place's box reaches, merged by id (`Network::absorb`). A tile holds every road reaching into its core widened by 0.5 km, so one tile covers an area's 0.4 km reach. The place is cut out of it with `Network::clip_to(&tile.frame, &area.bounds().frame())`. A place no tile holds is not covered (`place-not-covered`); there is no Overpass and no importer in the browser.
- A tile-made area is kept with its tile's checksum plus `cityloom_model::IMPORTER_VERSION`, raised whenever importer output changes for the same data, so a changed tile or importer has it read again (7 reads each road's speed, 8 keeps the way a timed bus lane runs).
- `CityStore::current` opens the chosen area (`cityloom-area`), else the default (Plateau Mont-Royal, made like any other); until its roads are kept the city has no places. The network is kept under `cityloom-network:<key>`. Edits are not kept per area.

## Persistence
- A page opened from the map (`?junction=3`, `?street=7`) edits one place. `CityBinding` writes back through `CityStore` against the city as stored now (another tab's change is not overwritten), behind a 250 ms debounced `Keeper`; `flush()` on `pagehide`. Without a place of the city the page goes back to the map. A write failure is said once.
- `Arm.stops`, `Arm.beyond` and `Arm.section` are derived from the street (`#[serde(skip)]`), never kept. The positional uid (`?street=7`, `s-<uid>`) stays internal.

### The edits document (`cityloom-edits`, `city/edits.rs`; records keyed per `city/keys.rs`)
- Keys: `n<id>` for a junction (its smallest OSM node); `w<id>~n<id>~n<id>` for a street (smallest way, then its two ends' keys). An end that is no OSM node (road cut at the area's box) is `x`, and the key then ends with the way the street leaves its real end, in four compass words (`w7~n2~x~e`), measured from the layout node to the first point a metre along osm2streets' trimmed centreline.
- That word can flip between areas (osm2streets moves both when a junction's cluster changes with the box, up to 60 degrees on the Plateau); matching by ways, nodes and bearing then finds the street. Residual risk: an exact key, which outranks the bearing check, naming another piece after a flip (none seen over 11 probed areas). Proper fix: anchor the key on the next OSM node along the way.
- Halves of a closed way end their keys with the way each leaves its first end.
- A key two places still share is no key: such a place is never written (the write says it was not kept).
- A place is written alone: one record, in the document as kept (every other record, one this build cannot read too, is written back unchanged), replacing the record it took its edit from when that had another key.
- Matching a record to a place: by key, else by the OSM ways and nodes it was made from (of pieces overlapping it as much, the one leaving a shared end the same way; a piece leaving a shared end 45 degrees or more from the record's bearing does not overlap). A junction's arm finds its street by key, or a shared way leaving the same way. A street is matched on width (`row_mm`), not class (an edit changes the class; the record carries `class_before`).
- Records of places not in the area opened, or matched by none, are left as they are. An edit follows its place into another area/tile when the importer makes the place the same there (`citymodel_builder/tests/importer/real_city.rs`, Plateau tile shifted 150 m E/W/N/S: at least 95% of same-made places follow, none takes another's edit; `clip_agrees.rs` measures the same for the cut against a clipped import). Where osm2streets builds it otherwise (roads merged into another width, a junction with other arms) the record stays an orphan, retried at each load, with no UI yet.
- A document that cannot be read is copied to `cityloom-edits-damaged` (unless a copy exists) before the first write. One from a newer version is neither read nor written over. Version 3 or older is handled as damaged (copied unless a copy is kept, replaced on the first write). A test pins a record's written shape: a change to `Street` or `State` is a decision about `version`.
- With server storage the same records are also kept under the account (see `sync/`): `cityloom-sync` (what waits to be sent, the revision pulled up to), `cityloom-sync-damaged`; the edits keep their shape. Per-area saves of earlier builds (`cityloom-city:<area>`) are neither read nor moved.
- Accepted minors: a second damaged document is dropped when a copy exists; the golden test pins only the fields the sample records write (not `None`-skipped ones); loop halves leaving their first end within one quarter share a key and are refused.

### Document version (`Edits::written_version`, `Edits::to_json`)
- The version is what the features present in some street record need (first matching rule wins; none: `VERSION` 5), so a build without them reads the document as before. Applied also to records this build cannot read.
  - `VERSION` 5: base; adds `State.turbo` (additive, skipped when `None`); a version 4 document is read as is and written as 5.
  - `ZONES_VERSION` 6: street zones (`zones`, `from_end`, `length_mm`).
  - `SPEED_VERSION` 7: `speed_kmh` on a street record.
  - `CURB_VERSION` 8: `Street.curb`. `STOP_VERSION` 9: `Street.stops`.
  - `SIGNAL_VERSION` 10: a junction's edited signal plan.
  - `GREEN_VERSION` 11: `Street.green`.
  - `CALMING_VERSION` 12 (`GREEN_VERSION + 1`): `Street.calming`.
  - `ZONE_SPEED_VERSION` 13: a zone's own `speed_kmh` (`holds_zone_speed`).
  - `CHICANE_VERSION` 14: a chicane (`holds_chicane`).
  - `FILTER_VERSION` 15: a modal filter (`holds_filter`), the newest and the first rule of `version_for`.
- `NEWEST` is `FILTER_VERSION` (15). Versions 4 up to `NEWEST` are read; above it is neither read nor written over.
- An older build meeting a newer document leaves it alone; so does this build's sync (`sync::local` finds that kind not `usable` and says it is paused).
- Whichever of two open PRs taking the next number merges second renumbers its constant.
- Speed limit: `Street.speed` (`Speed::Undecided` | `NotSet` | `Kmh(n)`) is saved as `speed_kmh` only when the designer changed it from the import (`City::street_record` compares with `today_streets`): absent when equal (follows OSM `maxspeed` at each import); `null` for Not set against an OSM speed; a number for the designer's.
- A record with zones writes `zones`, `from_end`, `length_mm` (golden test pins the shape, `edits::tests` the version rule). 20 zones fit a keepalive batch (`sync::model::KEEPALIVE_BYTES`, 60 000 bytes; a test pins it), well inside the server's `MAX_RECORD_BYTES` (64 KB).
- **Known limit:** the sync carries single records, not the document. A tab on an older build (opened before the deploy) that receives a zoned street record and then edits that street writes it back through its own `sync::local::write` without `zones`, `from_end`, `length_mm`, and the loss is pushed to other devices. Same-browser tabs are safe (one stored document); only a stale tab on another device with sync on is exposed. Fix to come: carry zoned records under a sync kind older builds ignore.
