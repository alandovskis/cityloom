# Street slice (`src/street/`)

Loaded when working in this folder; the root `CLAUDE.md` has the project-wide rules.

The street's data types are `cityloom_model::street` (re-exported by `street::model`): a `Street`'s fields, `Segment`/`Variant`, `Zone`/`ZoneEnd`/`ZoneFrame`, `Speed`, `Instance`/`Use`, `Stop`/`StopKind`, `Calming`/`CalmingKind`, `Green`/`GreenKind`/`TreeSize`, `Piece`/`Window`, with `reversed`, `for_side`, `sections`, `settle_zones`, `settle_class`, `is_sound`, `zone_speed`, `at_end` and the soundness and reversal of each programme. A file under `street/model/` with a name below (`zones.rs`, `curb.rs`, `stop.rs`, `calming.rs`, `green.rs`, `zone_speed.rs`) holds the editor's part: the edits, checks, views, thresholds and message keys, the latter two on `…Ext` traits; `StreetExt` (`street/model/mod.rs`) holds `imported` (widths clamped to the standards profile), `title`, `beyond`, `end_uses`, `end_stops`, `map_uses`, `taper_speed_kmh` and, in tests, `sample`; `SegmentBounds` the widths and other kinds a piece may take.

Every strip/list on the page (pieces, curb, stops, green, calming) is a WAI-ARIA listbox: one tab stop, `aria-activedescendant`, keys from `keys::` (`on_strip`: arrows/Home/End choose, Alt+arrow moves 1 m, Shift+Alt 10 m). The rows run as the plan does, from its left end, whichever end is anchored: `strip_cells` lays a row's cells in that order (reversed on a street anchored at its last end; the Green row sorts its cells so, `GreenCell.index` naming the element), so the arrows, Home/End and "n of total" go left to right, Alt+arrow moves rightward on the plan (`StreetVm::rightward`), and a drag in an unfolded row follows the pointer (`drag_span`'s `mirrored`, a negative `mm_per_px`).

**Read-only (#222).** `StreetVm::read_only()` is the page's storage's (`Storage::read_only`, a page opened with `view=1`). Every edit command refuses first and says `read-only-refused` (shell's message): `apply` (so `SheetWatch::edit` and the key actions), `undo`/`redo`/`reset`, `apply_template`, `apply_measure`, `add_item`, `add_curb`/`add_stop`/`add_calming`, `move_chosen_boundary` (the Move to field), `nudge_pick_boundary` (`PlanKey::NudgeBoundary`), the begin of every drag, and the helpers `zone_edit`, `row_edit`, `calming_edit`, `green_edit`. Picking a stretch is choosing, not editing: the plan, its point and the pick's From/To fields (`#pick-slot`, not locked) still pick; `StreetVm::new` and the sync reload give the editor `with_fixed_boundaries(true)`, so every `PlanBoundary::movable` is false (drawn the same, no elsewhere notice) and the hit layer offers no boundary: a press beside one is on the street under the pointer (no grab offset), so a click picks the run there and a drag keeps its ends under the pointer; `choose_boundary` refuses all the same. The point's `aria-keyshortcuts` (`StreetVm::plan_shortcuts`) leave out the Alt+arrows there. Behind them `core_edit` puts back any change to the street's history that reaches the editor another way (`quiet`, `edit`: `Editor::same_history` compares revisions, cursor, uids and the drag under way, so a choice or the time of day stays), says `read-only-refused` and gives no result (`edit`/`quiet`: `R::default()`, so nothing is announced or recorded as an edit), and the keeper is never touched, so nothing is flushed or marked. `link` keeps `view=1` (`project_id::link_for`). `region_is_design` is true (the shell's `Target`; the map's and `Bare`'s are false, so a read-only map's region stays free) and `apply_region` takes no region and says nothing read-only (the drive side is the design; the shell's `choose_region` refuses with `read-only-refused`, writes no `cityloom-region` and the menu is disabled). The page: `History`, `AddMenu` and the Transform button (`transform::TransformDialog`; its `apply_choice` goes through `apply`/`apply_measure`/`apply_template`) are not drawn (`SheetWatch::read_only`); the tools panels and the inspector are mounted inside `shared::locked::locked` (a disabled `fieldset.read-only-controls`, `display: contents`), so they are shown greyed; the strips, piece list, path, plan and drawing still choose. The hint under the drawing (`#focus-hint`) gets `data-i18n` from `StreetVm::focus_hint_key` at mount: `st-focus-hint-read-only` (only the arrows) read-only.

## Stretches and runs (`street/model/zones.rs`, `pick.rs`, `matching.rs`, `replay.rs`)

- The saved form keeps zones (`Zone`, `zones.rs` are internal names; the page says stretches; `select_zone` and `follow_zone`, which chose a zone and picked its span, are test-only, and `ZoneView` keeps only a zone's place and the name of its run): `Street.zones: Vec<Zone>` (`Zone { from_mm, segments: Option<Vec<Segment>>, name, speed }`; `None` = base section; first zone at 0), `from_end` (`ZoneEnd`: end positions are measured from), `length_mm` (length drawn on). A saved name round-trips and is never shown.
- Canonical (`settle_zones`): one zone = empty `zones`, street unchanged in every visible and stored way; a zone equal to the base ignoring uids (`same_pieces`) is `None`.
- `MIN_ZONE_MM` 5 m; positions snap to 100 mm (`snap`). `ZoneRefusal`: `NoAnchor`, `Elsewhere`, `TooShort` (with minimum), `OutsideStreet`, `NoBoundary`, `Dragging`, said in the words of stretches and boundaries (`pick-refused-*`, `boundary-refused-elsewhere`, `zone-refused-dragging` "Finish the drag before changing the street.").
- **Wording:** no message of any slice says "zone" of a street (`zone_guard::no_message_anywhere_says_zone_except_school_and_loading_zones`, `src/zone_guard.rs`, over every slice's `RESOURCES`; its one `ALLOWED` list keeps "school zone", "loading zone" and their French forms, "zone tampon" and two area names, and a French message may say `zone` for a map area where its English says "area"): a zone is named by the stretch it covers, `stretch-mid` "from 49 m to 82 m" (`stretch_between`, `Editor::stretch_said(z)`, `Editor::stretch_of`, positions from the plan's left end via `left_mm`), never by a saved name; a boundary by where it is (`check-boundary` "where the street changes at 49 m", `rev-boundary-move`). The junction names the stretch beyond an end stretch with the same message, but in the arm's own frame, measured from the junction's centre (`Beyond.stretch`, `beyond.rs`): that page has no plan and no first end to see.
- **Anchoring:** from a real OSM node end (`ZoneFrame`, `Layout::zone_frame`); an end cut at the area's box is no anchor; both cut = no frame, no zone. A street with no frame names no positions: a stretch is `stretch-mid-whole` ("along the whole street") or `stretch-mid-part` (`Editor::stretch_of`), a boundary `check-boundary-somewhere`, a missed edit `edit-missed-part` once per kind (`text::missed_part_said`).
- **Elsewhere:** loaded length differs from `length_mm` by more than 1 m, or anchor is the other end: zones kept, notice `stretches-elsewhere`; a pick snaps out to the whole runs covering its ends (so an edit splits nothing and nothing is joined), a boundary move is refused (`boundary-refused-elsewhere`, also during a drag); sections stay editable.
- **Runs and the pick** (`pick.rs`): a run (`Run`) is a maximal part of neighbouring zones that look the same (pieces ignoring uids, and speed). The pick (`Pick::{Whole, Stretch}`, anchored mm) is outside history (undo/redo/Start over only clamp it); a press on the plan picks a run, a drag a stretch (snapped to 100 mm, and to a boundary or end within `MIN_ZONE_MM`; shorter refused, `PickRefusal`). The shown run (`Editor::zone()`, `shown_zone`) is the one at the pick's left end; the whole street shows the leftmost run, whichever end is anchored. A pick alone never splits anything.
- **An edit over the pick** (`Editor::over_pick`): splits zones at the pick's ends (`split_for_pick`), makes the edit on the shown run's section (refusals come from it alone), replays it onto every other run in the pick (`replay.rs`: the difference before/after made again on each piece's counterpart, `matching.rs`'s `counterpart`, by uid then by kind left to right, so a whole-street edit after the School template reaches its school stretch), reports the runs it `missed` (said `edit-missed`), then joins neighbours that now look the same (`Content::join_alike`, not on an elsewhere street) and settles; a selected piece the join took away (the later run's uids are gone) is its counterpart in the run shown (`follow_selected`), else nothing. Loading never joins (`settle_zones`, `Street::settled`, `Content::settle` keep their behaviour). Inside a 2.5D drag the split is made once, on the first changing step; the join and the one revision wait for `end_gesture`, and Escape leaves no boundary. A zone without its own section copies the base on first write.
- Uids are unique within a section, not across (copies keep uids): key per-piece things on the zone too.
- `split_zone` is `#[cfg(test)]`: tests build zoned streets with it (a named zone is built as a `Street` with `name: Some(..)`); there is no merge or naming command, no Zone level in the details panel, and the page has no zone commands (a stretch is picked on the plan, and an edit of the pick splits and joins runs). `move_boundary_of` stays: the plan's boundary drag and `PlanKey::NudgeBoundary` (`move_boundary` and `nudge_boundary`, the selected zone's, are test-only). It keeps the boundary `MIN_ZONE_MM` from the boundaries the plan shows, as `boundary_kept` keeps a drag; a boundary a saved street holds between zones that look the same (inside a run) is joined by the move rather than stopping it, so it gives back the zone the boundary starts afterwards (the drag's `PlanGesture::Boundary` and the chosen boundary follow it).
- **Readers:** checks run on every section (`Street::sections`), worst reported with the stretch named (`check-stretch-in`) against the baseline of the street as first laid out; class derived over all zones (pedestrian only if all are); area rule counts the largest conflict count in any one section; junction arm reads `Street::at_end`; street page reads the shown run (and the pick, for what varies across it); map pieces (`EdgeView.pieces`) are zone 0's.
- **2.5D across a boundary:** the shown run's stretch = first 40 m (`STRETCH_MM`). The joint is across a boundary between runs, never one hidden inside a run, and it and the shown zone (`ZoneView.label`) are named by their run's span. Tests that need a boundary with the same pieces either side build it with `zoned_apart` (a speed limit of its own per zone) or give a split zone a speed. `near_boundary`: boundary within `JOINT_REACH_MM` (20 m) of the stretch (end boundary if at most 20 m past the stretch's end, else start; end wins). Then `ZonesView.joint: Option<Joint>` (`segments`, `total_mm`, `far`, `at_mm`, `left_mm`, `zone`, `label` (the stretch); `SHOWN_MM`) carries the zone across it.
  - `iso_svg` with a joint: two 20 m runs `<g class="iso-section">`, neighbour's also `iso-across` (far iff `joint.far`), far run first, abrupt change, no taper (#86); neighbour pieces `iso-piece iso-across` without `data-uid`; layers take `&[SegView]` + a `Reach` (`Projection::over`, `band_edges(Reach)`). No joint near = byte-identical to 0.26.0 (no `iso-section`). Known limit: two sections meeting, not their lengths; selecting either zone gives the same picture.
- **Map:** `EdgeView.zones` = full zone count; `EdgeView.zone_breaks_mm` (from node `a` along `shape_mm`) only when the record's anchor matches the frame's and the boundary is in the loaded road. `overlay::zone_ticks` (source `zone-ticks`, layers `zone-ticks-casing`/`zone-ticks` from `style::zone_tick_layers`; `Mapper::set_zone_ticks`, `web/mapcanvas.js` `setZoneTicks`/`showZoneTicks`, re-shown on `style.load`): not muted with a non-editable street, no hit-test. No count of stretches is said: the ticks and bands show where a street changes (`map-label-street`, `map-street-sub` and their `-programme` forms); the report's street page says `map-report-place-zones` ("This street changes along its length. The figures show it at its first end.").

## Picking a stretch (`street/vm/plan.rs`, `keys::on_plan`, the component `view/plan/component.rs`)

- `vm/plan.rs` holds the pure press machine (`PlanPress`, `PlanGesture`, `press`, `moved`, `is_boundary_drag`'s 4 px) and, below it, `StreetVm`'s picking commands. They take positions from the plan's left end (the first end) and convert with `Editor::anchored_mm`: `pick_stretch`, `pick_run_at`, `pick_whole` (each says `stretch-picked`, `stretch-picked-differs` with what sets a whole run apart from the run on its left, else its right, from `Editor::pick_differs`, or `stretch-whole`; a refusal `pick-refused-*`), `picked()` (tracked), `plan_point()` and `plan_key(PlanKey)` (arrows 1 m, Page Up/Down 10 m, Home/End; Shift extends from where the extension began, silently until it is `MIN_ZONE_MM` long; Enter the run at the point; Escape the whole street; Alt+arrow moves the boundary at the pick's left end, else its right end, the pick's end following it, else `pick-refused-no-boundary`). The Street step of the path picks the whole street. On the details panel's Street level, under the street's head, `view::plan::PickFields` (`#pick-slot`: `#pick-from`, `#pick-to`, `st-pick-*`) shows the pick's ends (`pick_ends_shown`, 0 and the length for the whole street; none without an anchor) and a change in either (Enter, or leaving it) keeps what was typed as that field's draft (`type_pick_from`, `type_pick_to`, `PickDrafts`) and picks from the From draft, else the pick's left end, to the To draft, else its right end, never swapping them (`commit_pick_drafts`, `parse_length_mm`); refused (not a number, `pick-refused-order` "From must come before To.", or the pick's own refusals) the drafts stay as typed, shown in the fields, so the other field can put it right; a pick made any way (`pick_with`), a boundary move (`repick`), the history or a sync (`with_pick_note`) clears them: a single pointer picks part of a run without dragging (WCAG 2.5.7).
- A pick made with the pointer (a click, a drag, a handle, a lane) or typed takes the keyboard's point to its left end (`pointer_picked`), so the keys go on from it; a sync reload keeps the point on the street (`clamp_point`). A pick from end to end is the whole street, even on a street shorter than `MIN_ZONE_MM` (a drag, Shift+End).
- Pointer: the view keeps a `PlanGesture`: `plan_press` (a thing is chosen at once, its level asked), `plan_move` (past the threshold it becomes a pick, handle, boundary or thing drag), `plan_release` (a press not moved picks the run there), `plan_cancel`. A boundary drag moves its zone's start with `Editor::move_boundary_of` (the pick and the selected piece stay while it lasts; once let go, a pick end that was on it follows); a thing drag goes through its row's drag commands.
- A stretch picked chooses, on the curbside and stops rows (the side already chosen, else the side traffic keeps) and the raised elements' row, the stretch at its left end, so `add_curb`/`add_stop`/`add_calming` add at the pick's start; with the whole street picked, at the free stretch's left end on the plan. Either way the thing starts at a left end whichever end is anchored. Green keeps `add_green_clear`.
- **A boundary without a drag** (WCAG 2.5.7): a click (press and release, no drag) on a movable boundary chooses it (`StreetVm.boundary`, by the zone it starts, outside the history; `choose_boundary` says `boundary-chosen` "The change at 40.0 m; type where it goes.", asks the Street level and picks nothing; on an elsewhere street it says why and chooses nothing). `PickFields` then shows `#boundary-to` (`st-boundary-to`, `boundary_to_shown`); Enter there moves it (`move_chosen_boundary`, through `move_boundary_to`, which `PlanKey::NudgeBoundary` shares: `move_boundary_of`, the pick's end on it following, refusals said), Escape (`clear_boundary`), a press anywhere else on the plan, a drag of a boundary or Escape on the point lets it go (`chosen_boundary()` is also `None` once the boundary is gone).
- An edit over the pick that missed a run says `edit-missed` after its own words, naming the kind the run lacked (`Editor::missed` gives anchored spans with that kind).

## Transitions between zones (`street/model/transition.rs`)

Derived from the zones' sections and speed, never saved (`city::edits::VERSION` stays 5).

- `transition` matches pieces of two zones by uid, then by kind left to right, keeps the longest non-crossing set (a piece moved across others is the only one split into dropped + added), gives each change a taper; the longest rules the boundary. Uses the sections' own kinds, never a time-of-day variant's.
- Length rules, all UNVERIFIED (TAC GDG ch. 8-9, MTQ Tome I ch. 8 not checked); W width m, S speed km/h (50 if none), L rounded up to 100 mm:
  - shift/width change/dropped lane of travel, bus, parking, loading, shoulder: `W*S^2/155` below 70 km/h, `0.62*W*S` from 70 (US MUTCD 2003 Part 3 Fig. 3B-12, metric merge taper);
  - travel/bus lane added: `R*W`, R 15 up to 50 km/h, 18 up to 60, 24 above (York Region DS-100, 2023, from TAC);
  - lane added/dropped by its own direction of travel, not by which zone has it: only in the zone it travels into = opens (`R*W`); only in the zone it travels out of = ends (merge taper); so a pocket has the same length at either end whichever end is anchored; no direction (two-way) = merge taper, the longer;
  - bike lane `5*W` (NACTO, at least 1:5); sidewalks, planting, medians, buffers, terraces: none; a lane added mid-section also counts its neighbours' shift.
- Host: zone lacking the added/dropped piece (both: fewer roadway pieces, else farther from the anchored end); neither: zone farther from the anchored end.
- With any transition, two checks follow the zone checks (one failing check per failing boundary, else one passing):
  - `transition-room` ("Room for the transition"): host shorter than the taper; fails the host zone in the strip.
  - `lane-drop` ("Lane drops"): a travel/bus lane ending at the boundary needs a lane of its direction beside it, in its own section, that the other side has too; lanes that open, have no direction, or that the other side lacks entirely need no merge. `Segment.direction` 0 "away" runs first end to last, so near-to-far is 0 when `from_end` is `First`, else 1; hence `transition` takes the `ZoneEnd`.
- Same answer whichever end is anchored; none for a zoning on a road of another length (`Editor::elsewhere`); the map's editor gets the street's frame so its failing list and status count agree with the page.
- 2.5D: schematic taper (`Joint.taper`, `street/view/iso/taper.rs`, `iso-taper`) fills the host's half of the 40 m stretch whatever the true length; the label gives the true one.
- Map: a boundary with a transition gets a band, not a tick: `EdgeView.zone_bands_mm` `(boundary index, start, end)`; `map::vm::overlay::zone_ticks` emits a LineString with `band: true` into the same source/layers; `zone_bands` draws none for a zoning on a road of another length by more than 1 m (tick stays). The junction draws them at true scale (root file, **Junction approaches**).
- Known limits: taper schematic; raised pieces (sidewalks, curbs, a bike lane's curb strip) stay in the host's place; selected piece's outline stays the untapered rectangle and a tapered top loses joints; label rounds to the metre vs the check's "52.5 m"; host follows anchored order, not traffic direction; a zone hosting two transitions is checked against each separately (two tapers in one short zone can overlap).

## The 2.5D drawing (`street/view/iso/`; #139)

The page's only drawing (the cross-section view `street/svg.rs`, `ViewMode` and `V` are gone: no handles/drags, width and order are edited in the inspector and with keys, no mode toggle). Shared with the importer: `street/model/pieces.rs` (`curb_edges`, `curb_width_mm`, `ROAD`, `dir_glyph`). A press on a piece (`data-uid` on its `iso-piece` group; a lifted curbside strip carries its piece's uid) selects it.

- **Projection:** perspective from over the centre line (`iso::Projection`, not `city::projection`'s): 40 m stretch (`STRETCH_MM`), eye `EYE_HEIGHT_MM` (9 m) up, `EYE_BACK_MM` (14 m) behind the near end; scale at `along` mm = `EYE_BACK_MM / (EYE_BACK_MM + along)`. Width down to `MIN_WIDTH` (280 px, no sideways scroll); `max_height(width)` (484 px scaled by width, 0.9 to 1.1) keeps the status line in view at 1440x900; group `min-height` is `iso::max_height`, it carries `iso-view`, followed by `watch_size`.
- **Heights/solids:** `HEIGHTS` (`curb_height_mm`); slab rises (sidewalk, median, planting, curbs) drawn `RISE` (3) times as high, a curb above its own piece at true height (`curb_top_mm`); `TALLEST_MM` (5.5 m) is the highest anything is drawn (a test guards it). Boxes (`Solid`: tree trunk/crown, shelter, curb post) too narrow on screen at their own depth are dropped: `MIN_STANDING_PX` (3 px) for a box, `MIN_SOLID_PX` (20 px) for a symbol (at `ARROW_AT_MM`).
- **Diagram:** `street::view::iso::scene` builds each layer as `shared::diagram` shapes (`Pt` in ground mm `(across, up, along)`); `mod.rs` `diagram()`/`section()` assembles a `Diagram`, `shared::diagram::render` writes SVG through the street's `Projection`. Builders in `scene/`: `surface`, `markings`, `raised`, `objects`, `signs`, `outline`, `hits`, `tables`; helpers `projection.rs`, `raised.rs`, `joints.rs`, `order.rs`, `solids.rs`, `symbols.rs`, `paint.rs`, `sign.rs`, `curbside.rs`, `stops.rs`, `taper.rs`.
- **Sections and layers:** a `Section` is one run over a `Reach` (no joint: one, no class; joint: two, `iso-section` and `iso-section iso-across`, far first), each with five layers. Order: `<defs>`; `iso-layer-ground` (one `iso-ground` polygon); per section `iso-surface` (roadway tops, each an `iso-piece` with `data-uid`), `iso-markings`, `iso-raised` (strips above the roadway, after markings so a sidewalk/curb covers paint), `iso-objects` (bare `iso-solid`), `iso-signs`; last `iso-layer-outline` (`iso-sel`), then hits.
- **Faces and order:** `raised::inner_face` (a piece left of the eye shows its inner face where the neighbour is lower, right of the eye its left face, over the eye neither); `order::paint_order` (a box paints after another when a separating plane has it on the eye's side: nearer the centre line, nearer the near end, or higher); strips keep `raised::strips` order. **Accepted exception:** objects paint over all raised strips of their section, not interleaved, so a strip nearer the middle no longer covers the foot of a post beyond it.
- Turned faces are shaded by two SVG filters in the drawing's own `<defs>` (`SHADES` in `street/view/iso/raised.rs`: 0.9 front, 0.78 side, sRGB, `shared::iso::shading`), not CSS `filter: brightness()` (Safari may not apply it to SVG; unchecked).
- Screen-reader label `svg-iso-label`/`iso_label` (not the speed limit: that is at the details panel's Street level); heading `page::DrawingHeading` (`#h-drawing`). `.focus-hint` keeps its room when hidden (`visibility`, not `display`, toggled on `.wrap:focus-visible`), else the scroller clamps and a press on a strip below the fold lands on its neighbour.
- **Press targets** (`scene/hits.rs`, `LayerKind::Hit`, `Diagram.hits`, `iso-layer-hit`): last layer, after the outline, outside the sections; per piece of the selected run an `iso-hit` group (`data-uid`, kind name as `<title>`) with one unpainted `iso-hit-area` polygon, the silhouette (`outline::silhouette`), stroked 10 px (`fill-opacity: 0`, `stroke: transparent`, `pointer-events: all` in `web/editor.css`), widest first. Written only when it has shapes; markup pins (`testing::seen`) read it without the layer. Selection is the `click` (`pick` in `street/view/mod.rs`); `#drawing.iso` has `touch-action: manipulation`. Hover/press tint `--iso-sel` (0.3 / 0.55) and band `--iso-sel-casing` (0.45 / 0.75), measured in `shared/contrast.rs`; `Highlight` in forced colours.
- **Piece pane** (`street/view/inspector.rs`, `#inspector`, the details panel's Piece level): the name and "3.0 m wide · 2 of 6 from the left" (`inspector-sub`), then `#piece-move-left` / `#piece-move-right` / `#piece-remove` (the same `Action::Move` / `Action::Remove` as Shift and an arrow and Delete on the drawing; a move is disabled at either end; the position buttons removed in b91a7352 are back since the 2.5D has no drag), then one `.insp-sec` per setting laid out as a label column beside its control (`.street-page #inspector`), the width with `.insp-meter` (its place in the allowed range, `meter_pct`, `aria-hidden`: the range is said under it) and, when the piece's width differs between its counterparts in the picked runs (`SegView.varies`, `Editor::widths_vary`), `#i-width-varies` `st-varies` under the range (both describe the field), the radio groups (surface, direction, curb) as tiles three across. The junction's inspector keeps its own look.
- **Piece list** (`street/view/piece_list.rs`, `#pieces-slot`, `#piece-list`, under the status bar before the plan): listbox "Pieces, left to right" (`st-pieces-label`), cell per piece (`#piece-cell-<uid>`; `text::piece_text`), `keys::piece_target` (no wrap); a key or click makes the same `StreetVm::select` call as a press, so pieces the picture hides stay reachable. Cells at least 24 px both ways.
- **Markings** (`markings(v)`, pure function of the `View`, inferred from neighbouring pieces after MTQ Tome V and its Manuel technique sur le marquage routier); lane symbols at `ARROW_AT_MM` (`symbols.rs`; sizes estimated pending the official Répertoire des dispositifs), dropped on a piece narrower than `MIN_SOLID_PX` there. Paint is `iso-paint white|yellow` (never `iso-top`/`iso-front`/`iso-side`), `--iso-paint-white`/`--iso-paint-yellow` (0.5 px edge in the light theme keeps 3:1), `CanvasText` in forced colours, 3:1 on every material via a dark hairline (a test reads the tokens); not stored. Known limit: a roadway piece's hover outline is hidden where paint covers its edge.
- **Materials/textures:** a top takes its piece's surface material, not its kind's (`mat-<material>`, `--mat-*` in `web/app.css`, tones `--mat-*-line`/`-alt`, texture `url(#iso-m-<material>)`); faces and curbs keep the kind's tint/hatch (`k-*`, `h-*`); a junction surface that is no street piece takes `KINDS[..].materials[0]`. ## Speed limit on the street page

- `SpeedLimit` (`street/view/speed_view.rs`, `#speed-slot`, the details panel's Street level): `<select id="speed-limit">`: "Not set" first (value empty), then `SPEED_CHOICES` (10 to 120 km/h, steps of 10). `Editor::set_speed`: one undoable edit (`rev-speed`/`rev-speed-not-set`); refuses a value outside the choices (an imported speed outside them is kept and shown, never set) and a speed already set. Section edits do not change it; undo/Start over restore it; the select keeps focus.
- **A zone has its own speed** (#148; `street/model/zone_speed.rs`): `Zone.speed` (`Speed`), saved as `speed_kmh` (left out when `Undecided` = follows `Street.speed`, as `segments: None` is the base section; a number, or `null` for no limit of its own). `Editor::set_speed` sets the pick's (`rev-stretch-speed`, `rev-stretch-speed-not-set`, naming the picked stretch; `rev-speed` for the whole street) and `Editor::follow_street_speed` restores following (`rev-stretch-speed-street`); the select (`SpeedOption.street`, value `street`, `st-speed-street*`) offers "Same as the street: N km/h" first. One zone: the street's.
- Canonical form (`settle_zones`): a zone speed equal to the street's is `Undecided`; the one zone left after a merge gives its speed to the street; splitting copies the speed to both halves.
- **Read by:** the table's speed check in every zone the table overlaps (too-fast zone named); a taper, by the higher of its two zones' speeds (`taper_speed_kmh`, `transitions`, `Beyond`, the map's bands); an area's Speed limit rule, by the fastest zone (`max_speed_kmh`); `Street::at_end` for junction arms. `View.speed_kmh` = the shown run's, `View.street_speed_kmh` = street's, `View.speed_follows`.
- **Saved** as `ZONE_SPEED_VERSION` 13 (`CALMING_VERSION` + 1) only while a zone holds a `speed_kmh` key (`holds_zone_speed`; after the chicane's 14 in `version_for`). `e2e/tests/speed.spec.ts`.

## Raised tables and crossings (`street/model/calming.rs`, `calming_shown.rs`, `street/view/calming_strip.rs`, `calming_tools.rs`, `street/view/iso/calming.rs`, `street/view/iso/scene/tables.rs`; #147 in two PRs, part of #77, which also brought the school-zone template (#148) and the modal filters (#149))

- `Street.calming`: `Calming { from_mm, length_mm, kind }`, saved as `calming` (skipped when empty), curbside/stop conventions (`from_mm` from the anchored end, 100 mm snap, ordered, `NoAnchor`/`Elsewhere`/`Dragging`, one revision per edit). `Kind`: `Table` (whole roadway raised between its curbs, a ramp each end) or `Crossing` (a table with a zebra on its plateau); Kind select values are indexes of `Kind::ALL`. `length_mm` is ramp to ramp. No side, no piece: lifts every roadway piece drawn flat, in the selected zone.
- Refusals (`CalmingRefusal`, `calming-refused-*`): no anchor, elsewhere, dragging, outside the street, overlapping another element, shorter than `MIN_TABLE_MM`, a freeway, no roadway, a roadway changing with the clock (`TimeWindows`).
- Four checks, only with an element, judged in every zone it overlaps: `calming-length` (at least `MIN_TABLE_MM`, ramps plus a `MIN_PLATEAU_MM` plateau; a zone with no roadway fails), `calming-speed` (at most `MAX_TABLE_SPEED_KMH`; no speed is a note, never a fail), `calming-stop` (no overlap with a stop's boarding area and `CALMING_STOP_CLEAR_MM` clear, naming the stop; a table may cross a bus lane and stand near a stop), `calming-junction` (`CALMING_JUNCTION_CLEAR_MM` clear of the junction's reach, as `stop-junction`, from `EndInfo.reach_mm`).
- **UI:** `CalmingStrip` (`#calming-strip`, cells `#calming-<i>`) under the stops rows, drag as the other rows (`CalmingRow`; one undoable step, Escape cancels, refuses zone/curb/stop drags). An element is **added from the Add a piece menu** (`view::add_menu::AddMenu`, its own topic "Traffic calming", `group-calming`, items `.add-item`, one per `Kind::ALL`; greyed with `View.unavailable.calming`, `add-calming-no-anchor` (not `calming-refused-no-anchor`, which names raised tables alone), where the strip has no cell): a kind adds an element of its `default_mm` at the start of the chosen free stretch (`pick_calming_kind` then `add_calming`; none chosen says so). `CalmingTools` (`#calming-kind`, `#calming-length`, `#calming-remove`, and `#calming-first` for a chicane; no Add button) is the chosen element's settings: it is shown only while an element (not a free stretch) is chosen, like a piece's, and Enter in `#calming-length` resizes it. Chosen element is outside history (`Editor.calming_sel`).
- **2.5D** draws `View.calming_shown` (`ShownCalming`: `along`, `plateau`, `full`) over the shown run only: an `iso-table` group per element (name as title, no `data-uid`, not pressable: the strip selects it) with a ramp at each end of each roadway piece (`iso-ramp`, face `ramp`, shade `iso-shade-ramp`, in the defs only when a table is drawn) and the top at `TABLE_HEIGHT_MM` drawn `RISE` times as high; a crossing adds `iso-zebra` bars (the junction's paint). No lane markings between the ramp feet, no symbol on a table. Whole or not at all, judged on the plateau (`ShownCalming::drawn`); a transition's taper is not followed. Label `svg-iso-calming`. A street with no element draws byte-identically (a test pins twelve streets by hash).
- **Chicanes** (`Kind::Chicane`, appended last so the select values keep their place): staggered build-outs from alternate curbs along `length_mm`, which make the road bend. `Calming.first: Option<Side>` (the section's side of the first build-out; `None` is the right; saved only when set, kept only for a chicane, dropped by a change of kind). `Calming::buildouts()` gives them (`CHICANE_BUILDOUT_MM` 4 m long, `CHICANE_GAP_MM` 6 m apart, alternating, filling the length from its start).
- `Kind::min_mm` / `default_mm` / `max_kmh` give per kind the minimum length (`MIN_CHICANE_MM`, two build-outs and the gap; shorter is `TooShort`), the typed-length default (`DEFAULT_CHICANE_MM`) and the speed ceiling (`MAX_CHICANE_SPEED_KMH`, 40, per zone through `speed_in`; a table's is 50). Checks are the table's with the chicane's minimum (`check-detail-chicane-length`) plus a fifth, `calming-lane` (only with a chicane): a bus or bike lane in any zone it overlaps fails it, the zone named, since a chicane narrows the roadway to one path; stop and junction clearances are shared.
- `Editor::add_calming_first` and `set_chicane_first` (`rev-chicane-first`) set the side; `CalmingTools` shows a "Starts on" select (`#calming-first`) for the chicane kind only (`StreetVm::calming_first_shown` / `pick_calming_first`).
- The 2.5D draws each build-out wholly in the stretch as an `iso-chicane` group (the chicane's name as title, no `data-uid`): a planted block `CHICANE_WIDTH_MM` (2 m) into the roadway from the edge of the lifted pieces on its side, `CHICANE_HEIGHT_MM` high (drawn `RISE` times as high), ordered among the raised boxes (`ShownCalming.buildouts`, `iso/calming.rs`'s `Buildout`, `scene/raised.rs`'s `buildout`). The road is not drawn bending, the markings are not cut and a chicane has no ramps; the label counts them apart (`svg-iso-chicane`) and the ramps' shade stays for tables.
- **Saved** as `CHICANE_VERSION` 14 (`ZONE_SPEED_VERSION` + 1) only while a street record holds a chicane (`holds_chicane`, above the other rules of `version_for`). Tables and crossings alone stay at `CALMING_VERSION` 12 (`SIGNAL_VERSION` + 2; 11 is kept for #127's green) while a street record holds `calming` (`holds_calming`; after the zone speed's 13 and the chicane's 14 in `version_for`), so a build before chicanes still reads them. The sync needs no change.
- **Modal filters** (#149, street-level only; `Kind::ModalFilter`, appended last): a barrier or planter across the roadway that stops motor traffic and leaves a way through for people on foot and on bikes. No side and no `first`; `MIN_FILTER_MM` 1 m (shorter is `TooShort`), `DEFAULT_FILTER_MM` 2 m, `FILTER_HEIGHT_MM` 600 mm. `Kind::max_kmh` is an `Option<u8>` and a filter has none, so `calming-speed` skips it and the note's ceiling is the lowest of the other kinds present.
- Two rows exist only with a filter (rules 5 and 6 in `calming_failures`): `calming-filter-bus` (a bus lane in any zone it overlaps fails it, the zone named; a bike lane may pass through the gap, unlike under a chicane) and `calming-passage` (the zone needs a sidewalk or a bike lane so that people on foot or on bikes can get past). With `calming-length` (`check-detail-filter-length`) and the shared stop and junction clearances.
- The 2.5D: `ShownCalming::drawn` is `whole` for a filter; `iso/calming.rs`'s `Filter` and `Laid::filters` are laid across `filter_across`, the span of the motor pieces (`catalogue::is_motor`) among the lifted ones, so a bike lane or sidewalk beside them is left clear; `scene/raised.rs`'s `filter` draws an `iso-filter` group (name as title, no `data-uid`, planting like a build-out) ordered among the raised boxes, with no ramp and no ramp shade; the label counts them apart (`svg-iso-filter`).
- **Saved** as `FILTER_VERSION` 15 (`CHICANE_VERSION` + 1) only while a street record holds a filter (`holds_filter`, the first rule of `version_for`); chicanes, tables and crossings alone keep 14 and 12. Known limits: street-level only, no network model, so nothing says what a filter does to motor connectivity (no routing, no cut-off streets; a separate issue); a filter on a street with a bike lane between two motor lanes is drawn across it; one near a junction is judged on the street page only. All its figures are placeholders, UNVERIFIED.
- Figures UNVERIFIED (every chicane figure too): table height 150 mm and ramp 1.2 m (Montréal's raised-crossing sheet: 150 mm, 12.5 % ramps), minimum 6 m with 3 m plateau, 50 km/h, 5 m from a stop and from the junction, default 7 m, zebra bars 400 mm.
- Known limits: no map overlay; no junction awareness (an element near a junction is drawn and judged on the street page only); no plan drawing; the ramps' shade is one filter for every material.
