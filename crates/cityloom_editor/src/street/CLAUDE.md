# Street slice (`src/street/`)

Loaded when working in this folder; the root `CLAUDE.md` has the project-wide rules.

Every strip/list on the page (zones, pieces, curb, stops, green, calming) is a WAI-ARIA listbox: one tab stop, `aria-activedescendant`, keys from `keys::` (`on_strip`: arrows/Home/End choose, Alt+arrow moves 1 m, Shift+Alt 10 m).

## Street zones (`street/model/zones.rs`, `zone_tools.rs`, `strip.rs`)

- `Street.zones: Vec<Zone>` (`Zone { from_mm, segments: Option<Vec<Segment>>, name }`; `None` = base section; first zone at 0), `from_end` (`ZoneEnd`: end positions are measured from), `length_mm` (length drawn on).
- Canonical (`settle_zones`): one zone = empty `zones`, street unchanged in every visible and stored way; a zone equal to the base ignoring uids (`same_pieces`) is `None`.
- `MIN_ZONE_MM` 5 m; positions snap to 100 mm (`snap`). `ZoneRefusal` (said `zone-refused-*`): `NoAnchor`, `Elsewhere`, `TooShort` (with minimum), `OutsideStreet`, `NoNeighbour`, `NoBoundary`, `Dragging`.
- **Anchoring:** from a real OSM node end (`ZoneFrame`, `Layout::zone_frame`); an end cut at the area's box is no anchor; both cut = no frame, no zone.
- **Elsewhere:** loaded length differs from `length_mm` by more than 1 m, or anchor is the other end: zones kept, notice `zones-elsewhere`, every structure edit (split, merge, move boundary) refused (also during a drag); sections stay editable.
- Selected zone (`Editor::select_zone`) is outside history (undo/redo/Start over only clamp it). Piece edits go to it; a zone without own section copies the base on first write. `use_for_street` makes its section the street's; `reset_zone` gives it the base.
- Uids are unique within a section, not across (copies keep uids): key per-piece things on the zone too.
- Merging zones with different sections asks which to keep (`merge_needs_choice`, `Keep`); an impossible merge is refused before the question.
- **Readers:** checks run on every section (`Street::sections`), worst reported with the zone named (`check-zone-in`) against the baseline of the street as first laid out; class derived over all zones (pedestrian only if all are); area rule counts the largest conflict count in any one section; junction arm reads `Street::at_end`; street page reads the selected zone; map pieces (`EdgeView.pieces`) are zone 0's.
- **2.5D across a boundary:** selected zone's stretch = first 40 m (`STRETCH_MM`). `near_boundary`: boundary within `JOINT_REACH_MM` (20 m) of the stretch (end boundary if at most 20 m past the stretch's end, else start; end wins). Then `ZonesView.joint: Option<Joint>` (`segments`, `total_mm`, `far`, `at_mm`, `zone`, `label`, `label_mid`; `SHOWN_MM`) carries the zone across it.
  - `iso_svg` with a joint: two 20 m runs `<g class="iso-section">`, neighbour's also `iso-across` (far iff `joint.far`), far run first, abrupt change, no taper (#86); neighbour pieces `iso-piece iso-across` without `data-uid`; layers take `&[SegView]` + a `Reach` (`Projection::over`, `band_edges(Reach)`). No joint near = byte-identical to 0.26.0 (no `iso-section`). Known limit: two sections meeting, not their lengths; selecting either zone gives the same picture.
- **Map:** `EdgeView.zones` = full zone count; `EdgeView.zone_breaks_mm` (from node `a` along `shape_mm`) only when the record's anchor matches the frame's and the boundary is in the loaded road. `overlay::zone_ticks` (source `zone-ticks`, layers `zone-ticks-casing`/`zone-ticks` from `style::zone_tick_layers`; `Mapper::set_zone_ticks`, `web/mapcanvas.js` `setZoneTicks`/`showZoneTicks`, re-shown on `style.load`): not muted with a non-editable street, no hit-test. Count shown only above one zone (`map-label-street-zones`, `map-zones`, `map-street-sub-zones`).

## Transitions between zones (`street/model/transition.rs`)

Derived from the zones' sections and speed, never saved (`city::edits::VERSION` stays 5).

- `transition` matches pieces of two zones by uid, then by kind left to right, keeps the longest non-crossing set (a piece moved across others is the only one split into dropped + added), gives each change a taper; the longest rules the boundary. Uses the sections' own kinds, never a time-of-day variant's.
- Length rules, all UNVERIFIED (TAC GDG ch. 8-9, MTQ Tome I ch. 8 not checked); W width m, S speed km/h (50 if none), L rounded up to 100 mm:
  - shift/width change/dropped lane of travel, bus, parking, loading, shoulder: `W*S^2/155` below 70 km/h, `0.62*W*S` from 70 (US MUTCD 2003 Part 3 Fig. 3B-12, metric merge taper);
  - travel/bus lane added: `R*W`, R 15 up to 50 km/h, 18 up to 60, 24 above (York Region DS-100, 2023, from TAC);
  - lane added/dropped by its own direction of travel, not by which zone has it: only in the zone it travels into = opens (`R*W`); only in the zone it travels out of = ends (merge taper); so a pocket has the same length at either end whichever end is anchored; no direction (two-way) = merge taper, the longer;
  - bike lane `5*W` (NACTO, at least 1:5); sidewalks, planting, medians, buffers, terraces: none; a lane added mid-section also counts its neighbours' shift.
- Host: zone lacking the added/dropped piece (both: fewer roadway pieces, else farther from the anchored end); neither: zone farther from the anchored end.
- With any transition, two checks follow the zone checks (one failing check per failing boundary, else one passing):
  - `transition-room` ("Room for the transition"): host shorter than the taper; fails the host zone in the strip.
  - `lane-drop` ("Lane drops"): a travel/bus lane ending at the boundary needs a lane of its direction beside it, in its own section, that the other side has too; lanes that open, have no direction, or that the other side lacks entirely need no merge. `Segment.direction` 0 "away" runs first end to last, so near-to-far is 0 when `from_end` is `First`, else 1; hence `transition` takes the `ZoneEnd`.
- Same answer whichever end is anchored; none for a zoning on a road of another length (`Editor::elsewhere`); the map's editor gets the street's frame so its failing list and status count agree with the page.
- 2.5D: schematic taper (`Joint.taper`, `street/iso/taper.rs`, `iso-taper`) fills the host's half of the 40 m stretch whatever the true length; the label gives the true one.
- Map: a boundary with a transition gets a band, not a tick: `EdgeView.zone_bands_mm` `(boundary index, start, end)`; `map::overlay::zone_ticks` emits a LineString with `band: true` into the same source/layers; `zone_bands` draws none for a zoning on a road of another length by more than 1 m (tick stays). The junction draws them at true scale (root file, **Junction approaches**).
- Known limits: taper schematic; raised pieces (sidewalks, curbs, a bike lane's curb strip) stay in the host's place; selected piece's outline stays the untapered rectangle and a tapered top loses joints; label rounds to the metre vs the check's "52.5 m"; host follows anchored order, not traffic direction; a zone hosting two transitions is checked against each separately (two tapers in one short zone can overlap).

## The 2.5D drawing (`street/iso/`; #139)

The page's only drawing (the cross-section view `street/svg.rs`, `ViewMode` and `V` are gone: no handles/drags, width and order are edited in the inspector and with keys, no mode toggle). Shared with the importer: `street/pieces.rs` (`curb_edges`, `curb_width_mm`, `ROAD`, `dir_glyph`). A press on a piece (`data-uid` on its `iso-piece` group; a lifted curbside strip carries its piece's uid) selects it.

- **Projection:** perspective from over the centre line (`iso::Projection`, not `city::projection`'s): 40 m stretch (`STRETCH_MM`), eye `EYE_HEIGHT_MM` (9 m) up, `EYE_BACK_MM` (14 m) behind the near end; scale at `along` mm = `EYE_BACK_MM / (EYE_BACK_MM + along)`. Width down to `MIN_WIDTH` (280 px, no sideways scroll); `max_height(width)` (484 px scaled by width, 0.9 to 1.1) keeps the status line in view at 1440x900; group `min-height` is `iso::max_height`, it carries `iso-view`, followed by `watch_size`.
- **Heights/solids:** `HEIGHTS` (`curb_height_mm`); slab rises (sidewalk, median, planting, curbs) drawn `RISE` (3) times as high, a curb above its own piece at true height (`curb_top_mm`); `TALLEST_MM` (5.5 m) is the highest anything is drawn (a test guards it). Boxes (`Solid`: tree trunk/crown, shelter, curb post) too narrow on screen at their own depth are dropped: `MIN_STANDING_PX` (3 px) for a box, `MIN_SOLID_PX` (20 px) for a symbol (at `ARROW_AT_MM`).
- **Diagram:** `street::iso::scene` builds each layer as `shared::diagram` shapes (`Pt` in ground mm `(across, up, along)`); `mod.rs` `diagram()`/`section()` assembles a `Diagram`, `shared::diagram::render` writes SVG through the street's `Projection`. Builders in `scene/`: `surface`, `markings`, `raised`, `objects`, `signs`, `outline`, `hits`, `tables`; helpers `projection.rs`, `raised.rs`, `joints.rs`, `order.rs`, `solids.rs`, `symbols.rs`, `paint.rs`, `sign.rs`, `curbside.rs`, `stops.rs`, `taper.rs`.
- **Sections and layers:** a `Section` is one run over a `Reach` (no joint: one, no class; joint: two, `iso-section` and `iso-section iso-across`, far first), each with five layers. Order: `<defs>`; `iso-layer-ground` (one `iso-ground` polygon); per section `iso-surface` (roadway tops, each an `iso-piece` with `data-uid`), `iso-markings`, `iso-raised` (strips above the roadway, after markings so a sidewalk/curb covers paint), `iso-objects` (bare `iso-solid`), `iso-signs`; last `iso-layer-outline` (`iso-sel`), then hits.
- **Faces and order:** `raised::inner_face` (a piece left of the eye shows its inner face where the neighbour is lower, right of the eye its left face, over the eye neither); `order::paint_order` (a box paints after another when a separating plane has it on the eye's side: nearer the centre line, nearer the near end, or higher); strips keep `raised::strips` order. **Accepted exception:** objects paint over all raised strips of their section, not interleaved, so a strip nearer the middle no longer covers the foot of a post beyond it.
- Turned faces are shaded by two SVG filters in the drawing's own `<defs>` (`SHADES` in `street/iso/raised.rs`: 0.9 front, 0.78 side, sRGB, `shared::iso::shading`), not CSS `filter: brightness()` (Safari may not apply it to SVG; unchecked).
- Screen-reader label `svg-iso-label`/`iso_label` (not the speed limit: that is on the Street tab); heading `page::DrawingHeading` (`#h-drawing`). `.focus-hint` keeps its room when hidden (`visibility`, not `display`, toggled on `.wrap:focus-visible`), else the scroller clamps and a press on a strip below the fold lands on its neighbour.
- **Press targets** (`scene/hits.rs`, `LayerKind::Hit`, `Diagram.hits`, `iso-layer-hit`): last layer, after the outline, outside the sections; per piece of the selected run an `iso-hit` group (`data-uid`, kind name as `<title>`) with one unpainted `iso-hit-area` polygon, the silhouette (`outline::silhouette`), stroked 10 px (`fill-opacity: 0`, `stroke: transparent`, `pointer-events: all` in `web/editor.css`), widest first. Written only when it has shapes; markup pins (`testing::seen`) read it without the layer. Selection is the `click` (`pick` in `street/view.rs`); `#drawing.iso` has `touch-action: manipulation`. Hover/press tint `--iso-sel` (0.3 / 0.55) and band `--iso-sel-casing` (0.45 / 0.75), measured in `shared/contrast.rs`; `Highlight` in forced colours.
- **Piece list** (`street/piece_list.rs`, `#pieces-slot`, `#piece-list`, under the status bar before the zone strip): listbox "Pieces, left to right" (`st-pieces-label`), cell per piece (`#piece-cell-<uid>`; `text::piece_text`), `keys::piece_target` (no wrap); a key or click makes the same `StreetVm::select` call as a press, so pieces the picture hides stay reachable. Cells at least 24 px both ways.
- **Markings** (`markings(v)`, pure function of the `View`, inferred from neighbouring pieces after MTQ Tome V and its Manuel technique sur le marquage routier); lane symbols at `ARROW_AT_MM` (`symbols.rs`; sizes estimated pending the official Répertoire des dispositifs), dropped on a piece narrower than `MIN_SOLID_PX` there. Paint is `iso-paint white|yellow` (never `iso-top`/`iso-front`/`iso-side`), `--iso-paint-white`/`--iso-paint-yellow` (0.5 px edge in the light theme keeps 3:1), `CanvasText` in forced colours, 3:1 on every material via a dark hairline (a test reads the tokens); not stored. Known limit: a roadway piece's hover outline is hidden where paint covers its edge.
- **Materials/textures:** a top takes its piece's surface material, not its kind's (`mat-<material>`, `--mat-*` in `web/app.css`, tones `--mat-*-line`/`-alt`, texture `url(#iso-m-<material>)`); faces and curbs keep the kind's tint/hatch (`k-*`, `h-*`); a junction surface that is no street piece takes `KINDS[..].materials[0]`. Textures are pure Rust (`shared::iso::ground`, ground mm, `tile_mm`); the junction lays them by one `patternTransform` (`IsoFrame::at`); the street's perspective is not affine, so:
  - **Fine grain** (`fine_patterns`, no joints) is cut into `BANDS` (8) bands in equal steps of `1 / depth` (`band_edges`, `band_mid`), each with its own `transform` (`Projection::ground_at`) in `<g class="hatch">`.
  - **Regular joints** (`ground::joints`, a test pins the four: concrete 1.8 m panels, pavers' 125 mm courses with 500 mm stones, brick 100 mm courses of 200 mm bricks in running bond, tree-pit grate bars every 200 mm) are ground-mm lines projected point by point through `Projection::at` (`street/iso/joints.rs`, `<path class="joints j-<material>">`, width from `Joints` / `.joints.j-*` in `web/editor.css`), thinned below `MIN_JOINT_GAP_PX` (2 px) apart (`course_level`, `along_reach`).
  - `rain_garden` is `LAID_ONLY` (pattern in `<defs>` only when used, `iso::uses_material`).
- **Signs** (`street/iso/sign.rs`): no speed limit sign is drawn. `sign::Panel` (post + white panel) builds the stop sign (`stops.rs`); sizes (panel 600 by 750 mm) estimates pending MTQ Tome V; never drawn under `MIN_PANEL_PX` (32 px) wide: a smaller true size scales the whole sign up from its foot by one factor.

## Impact tab and Last change card (`street/impact.rs`, `notes::LastChange`, #64)

Notes panel's first tab (`#t-space`, shared with the junction's Streets tab; `ui-tab-impact`): the card (`#impact-card`, `#h-impact`, body `#impact`) above the width table and People moved.

- **Pure part** (no DOM/signals/words): `Input::of(&View, &[Finding])` reads width-table `outcomes.share` per `Mode`, design `checks` (`check_label`), area findings as `area-<n>`, the class, the history step's label; `diff(Cause, &before, &after)` gives `Impact { cause, headline, uses, class, now_passing, now_failing, verdict }`, all `Said` (`impact-*`, `st-impact-*`, plural selectors, `Arg::Length`).
- Only uses whose width changed are listed (`UseChange`, width-table order, buffer last). Newly passing: passes now and did not before, or no longer asked for and was failing; newly failing: fails now and did not before, or new and fails; a check appearing/going away passing is neither. `Verdict` (from those lists only): Improved (some pass, none fail), Regressed, Mixed, Unchanged. Class change adds `street-now-pedestrian`/`street-traffic-again`; nothing touched reads "No effect on space or checks".
- **A change** = the last announced edit only; a grouped operation (Transform, Atlas measure, Make pedestrian, zone or curbside command) is one step. Undo/Redo compare across the step (`Undone: <label>`/`Redone: <label>`), Start over = `Started over`; selecting, zone choice, drag steps make none. Compared: selected zone's checks (each worst over its zones) and, in an area, its area rules.
- **View-model:** `StreetVm::before()` takes the `Input` before every announced edit; `record` diffs after; `impact()`/`impact_now()`. Shown `IMPACT_DELAY_MS` (600 ms) after the last change via the `Scheduler` (each change cancels the timer); the edit announcement is untouched; a sync-reloaded street clears the card.
- **Card** is drawn from `StreetVm::impact()` only. Static markup in `street.html`: `aria-live="polite"`, `aria-atomic="true"` (never assertive), no focusable element inside. `.impact-verdict` adds `--ok`/`--warn`/`--ink` text with a 4 px bar in the text's colour (4.5:1 on the panel in both themes, `shared/contrast.rs`; `CanvasText` in forced colours).
- Known limits: switching language/units redraws the card (a screen reader may re-read it); a region change is not an edit, no summary; only the last change is kept (the Changes tab lists those).

## Curbside uses (`street/model/curb.rs`, `curb_shown.rs`, `street/curb_strip.rs`, `curb_tools.rs`, `street/iso/curbside.rs`; PR 1 of #85)

Row/selection/drag/strip code is shared with stops and calming: `street/row.rs` (`Row`, `CurbRow`, `StopRow`, `CalmingRow`), `StripView<C>`/`SideView<C>`, `StripCell`, `Choice`, `drag_span`, `curb_tools::typed_length`.

- `Street.curb`: `Instance { side, from_mm, length_mm, use }`, saved as `curb` (skipped when empty). `side` is the section's side (Left = first piece, whatever `from_end`); `from_mm` from the anchored end (`Street::from_end`; `reversed` swaps), snapped to 100 mm (`SNAP_MM`, also the minimum length). No real OSM end: `CurbRefusal::NoAnchor`; a programme for a road of another length or the other end is `Elsewhere` (shown, checked, not rearranged); a lane changing with the clock refuses (`TimeWindows`), checked over the whole run across every zone.
- Eight `Use`s in `Use::ALL` order (Use select values are indexes): parking, loading, street tree, planting, rain garden, patio, bike parking, transit platform; each drawn as a catalogue kind (`kind_id`: parking, loading, planting, terrace, bikerack, sidewalk); parking and loading stay on the roadway, the other six are raised (`Use::raised`).
- UNVERIFIED placeholders: `min_mm` (parking 5.5 m, loading and platform 9 m, tree/planting/bike parking 2 m, rain garden/patio 4 m), `CROSSING_CLEARANCE_MM` (5 m, parking/loading from a pedestrian crossing), `DOOR_BUFFER_MM` (500 mm, door into a bike lane: the MTQ's and Montréal's figure) and `PATIO_WALK_MM` (1.8 m of sidewalk a patio leaves: Montréal's local-street corridor). A shorter use is allowed and fails its check.
- Five checks (`curb-length`, `curb-lane`, `curb-crossing`, `curb-bike`, `curb-sidewalk`), each judged in every zone a use overlaps. The crossing check needs whether a crossing lies at each street end, given as `EndInfo` (`City::street_end_crossings` via `Editor::with_ends` in `City::street_editor` and `City::view`; `ZoneFrame` is only length and anchored end), so map and page agree. A failing row names its use (`check-curb-in`).
- **Strip/tools:** `CurbStrips`: a listbox per side with a curbside lane or uses (`#curb-strip-left|right`, under the zone strip), cell per use and free stretch (`#curb-{left|right}-{i}`). The chosen cell is a side and a place, not an index (`Editor.curb_sel`, outside history; a move is also written on the state, `State.curb_move`, so undo/redo/cancelled drag carry it). A press selects, a few pixels drags (`begin_curb_drag`, `drag_curb_to`, `end_curb_drag`): one undoable step, Escape cancels, the strip holds the pointer, only into adjacent free stretches. `CurbTools` (`#curb-use`, `#curb-length`, `#curb-add`, `#curb-remove`) adds (typed length, else minimum) at the start of the chosen free stretch, changes/removes the chosen; `curb-refused-*`; zone and curb commands refuse each other during any drag.
- **2.5D** draws `View.curb_shown` (`ShownInstance`: the part of each use in the 40 m stretch, in the section's direction): parking/loading in their lane's place, a raised use as its own strip (`lift`, hovered as the use, follows a transition's taper), a tree whole or not at all, bike racks as posts (`Solid::Post`, every metre). Label `svg-iso-curb`. Timed lanes draw none. Known limits: rack/tree positions schematic; a platform's crossing over a bike lane is not drawn.
- **At junctions** (`street/model/curb_end.rs`, `junction/curbside.rs`; derived, never saved: `Arm.curb_near` is `#[serde(skip)]` like `Arm.section`/`beyond`/`stops`, set by `curb_from` in `city/model.rs`; `city::edits::VERSION` stays 5):
  - `Street::end_uses(first_end, frame)` gives an arm `EndUse { index, side, use_, near_mm, far_mm, piece }` per use with a part in its end zone, nearest first, in the arm's orientation (mm from the junction centre, `side` swapped at the last end, cut at the zone boundary, `piece` the section's `curbside` piece or `None`). Empty without a frame, for a frame of another length/anchor (same gate as `end_stops` and the map), or without uses.
  - `junction::curbside::draw`: one strip per use on its piece in the use's kind/material (`Use::kind_id`, `Use::material`), cut to the drawn arm (`ARM_LENGTH_MM`) and the piece's strip, starting where the piece's strip does (parking/loading where the arm's parking piece begins, 1 m past the crossing; others at the crossing's far edge), so a nearer use draws nothing. Pressed as the arm; failing = `.curb-use-bad` (dashed ink, `Highlight` in forced colours); text `jn-sel-arm-curb`.
  - Check `curb-near` ("Curbside clear of crossings", `jn-check-curb-near*`, always listed) fails parking/loading whose near edge is less than `CROSSING_CLEARANCE_MM` (5 m, UNVERIFIED, the street page's constant) past the far edge of a drawn crossing (`jn-curb-near`, `jn-curb-near-inside`); no crossing, a pedestrian arm or a turbo's arm: nothing judged or drawn.
  - Known limits: end zone only; a use in a bike movement's path is not judged; placeholder figures; the map shows no uses for this check. `e2e/tests/junction.spec.ts`: parking at the centre (fails, nothing drawn), 30 m out (drawn, passes).

## Transit stops (`street/model/stop.rs`, `street/stop_tools.rs`, `street/iso/stops.rs`, `street/iso/sign.rs`, `solids::shelter`; PR 1 of #70)

- `Street.stops`: `Stop { side, from_mm, length_mm, kind, shelter, name }`, saved as `stops` (skipped when empty; `name` skipped when `None`), curbside conventions (`NoAnchor`/`Elsewhere`/`Dragging` from `curb_frame`, one revision per edit; name trimmed, blank cleared, cut at 80 characters). May overlap a curbside use, not another stop of its side.
- `StopKind::ALL` order (the Add a piece menu's Transit stops group lists them so): curb stop (bus stops in its lane at the sidewalk), curb extension (`Bulb`: platform where the parking/loading lane is), boarding island (`Island`: platform with a bike lane behind it, passing between island and sidewalk).
- **Bus lane** (`bus_lane`): the bus or driving lane nearest the edge on the stop's half that goes the way traffic goes there. Direction 0 runs first end to last; right-hand traffic: Right half carries 0, Left 1 (reversed for left-hand), whatever `from_end`. A stop on the half against traffic fails `stop-lane`.
- Six checks (`stop-lane`, `stop-kind`, `stop-length`, `stop-width`, `stop-bike`, `stop-shelter`; only with a stop; `Editor::stop_checks`), all but `stop-length` judged in every zone the stop overlaps; width/bike/shelter skipped where the lane or kind already fails. All figures UNVERIFIED (TAC / STM to check):
  - lane: a bus lane; kind build: curb = no motor piece between lane and edge, bulb = parking/loading before any other motor piece, island = parking/loading/buffer pieces then a bike lane;
  - length at least 8 m (`MIN_STOP_MM`: Montréal's platform for a single-unit bus; an articulated bus needs 16 m, not modelled);
  - boarding area 2.4 m (3.9 m with a shelter, 1.5 m of it the shelter's depth): curb = widest walkway outward (also counts a bike lane anywhere outward as crossed, a known looseness); bulb = the parking/loading piece plus the sidewalk run directly beyond (parking 2.1 m+ with sidewalk 1.2 m+ passes; a planting strip between breaks the run); island = filler pieces up to the bike lane;
  - bike: none across the boarding area (bulb: bike lane before the parking piece; curb: any between lane and edge, noting the crossing is not drawn; island: bike lane behind it at least 1.5 m).
  - Details are kind-specific (`check-detail-stop-width-curb|bulb|island`, etc.). On the Plateau's 1.5 m sidewalks a curb stop fails width; a curb extension with the sidewalk it joins gives 3.9 m+ where parking is 2.4 m+.
- A failing row names its stop (`check-stop-in`; `stop-instance`).
- **Rows/tools:** sr-only heading (`CurbStrips`) "Curbside uses and stops along the street" with both row kinds, "Stops along the street" with only stops. `StopRow`: a listbox per side where a bus has a lane in some zone or that holds a stop or curbside piece (`#stop-strip-left|right` under that side's curb row, caption "Stops, right"; cells `#stop-{left|right}-{i}`); drag as the curb row (`begin_stop_drag`; a stop jumping a neighbour stays chosen via `State.stop_move`; stop/curb/zone drags refuse each other).
