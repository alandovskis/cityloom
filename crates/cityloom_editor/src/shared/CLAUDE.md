# Shared kernel (`src/shared/`)

Loaded when working in this folder; the root `CLAUDE.md` has the project-wide rules.

- Kernel: catalogue, units, atlas, symbols. `shared` must not depend on any slice.
- Ports (fakes in `ports.rs`): `Announcer`, `Scheduler`, `Storage` (`recall`, `remember`, `keys`), `Mapper` (`MapEvent`, `NoMapper`, `FakeMapper`; `set_restrictions`, `set_draft`, `centre`, `set_insets`, `set_editable`; `MapEvent::View` reports the open view), `Fetcher`, `ArchiveFiles`, `Importer` (OSM reader, separate wasm module), `Account`, `Navigator`.
- `platform.rs`: browser side of the ports (`BrowserMapper` wraps `web/basemap.js`). `core.rs`: `Core<M: Presents>` = model + version signal + cached view; `view()` tracked, `view_now()` untracked (use in commands). Also `keeper.rs`, `shortcut.rs`, `bind.rs`, `testing.rs` (test-only HTML helpers).
- `diagram/`: layered drawing for the street's and junction's 2.5D (the junction is to adopt it later). Plain data in ground mm (`Pt`, `Shape`, `Layer`/`LayerKind`, `Section`, `Diagram`), `Projection` trait, `render` writes SVG.
- `iso/`: shared 2.5D parts: `format` (`esc`, `f1`), `shading` (`shade_filters`; each slice keeps its own ids), `paint` (ground-plane paint in mm, projection-independent: `quads` from polyline + `Style` (`Paint`, `Pattern`, `double`), `class` for CSS; line 120 mm, double gap 120 mm, dash 3 m, gap 6 m).

- **Sources behind the rules** (#78, PR 1; `sources.rs`, `sources_view.rs`): one table, `SOURCES` (a document: title, publisher, jurisdiction message, an `https` link only where one is confirmed) and `ENTRIES` (a rule's check id, its label message, where it applies, the source ids), read by every view and the report, so a citation is written once. `citation(rule)` never answers nothing: a rule with no entry is `Confidence::Placeholder` with the placeholder notice, so no check can be shown unmarked; an entry is `Confidence::Unchecked` (a source named, figures not checked; `Verified` is added the day a figure is checked). `note(rule, i18n, units)` words it and `source_note` draws it as a `<details class="src">` under each check row of the street and junction Checks lists (references open in a new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, with an accessible name that says so, so the editing context stays); the report's third page, Sources, lists the cited rules from `cited()`. Messages `src-*` are in `street/i18n`, the report's `map-report-sources*` in `map/i18n`. Cited today: the transition rules (`transition-room`, `lane-drop`: York DS-100 only; the US sources the formulas came from, MUTCD 2003 and NACTO, are deliberately not cited) and, from MTQ Tome I read in `the MTQ PDFs`, `curb-bike` (ch. 15 § 15.4.2.2: 0.5 m between a bike lane and parking, or a 2 m lane; CityLoom's `DOOR_BUFFER_MM` is 0.6 m and differs), `curb-crossing` and `curb-near` (ch. 14 § 14.3.2.1 and standard drawing 002: the last stall ends at least 5 m before the start of the corner's curb return, which matches `CROSSING_CLEARANCE_MM` but is measured from the curb return, not from the crossing), `curb-length` (standard drawing 001: stalls 6.7 to 7.5 m, first and last 6 to 6.5 m, for a 5 m vehicle; CityLoom's parking minimum of 5.5 m is lower and differs), `crossing` (ch. 8 § 8.10.2: a refuge island at least 2 m wide) and `green-sightline` (ch. 7 § 7.11.1: the visibility triangle); every other check is a placeholder. MTQ ch. 1 (functional classification) is in `SOURCES`, cited by no rule. MTQ ch. 8 has no mid-block zone taper (its tapers, § 8.9.3, are for turn lanes at intersections, 45 m typical urban), so the transition rules do not cite it. From the Ville de Montréal's Répertoire des pratiques d'aménagement de la rue (https://montreal-amenagement-rues.powerappsportals.com/, read on 2026-11-08; no sheet shows a date; the portal home is `mtl-repertoire`, cited by no rule): `calming-passage` (vélorue sheet: diverters leave a 1.8 m passage for cyclists both ways), `curb-bike` (vélorue: ideally 1 m beside a contra-flow lane; lane-width tool: 0.5 m where doors are a concern), `curb-crossing` and `curb-near` (visual-clearance tool: 5 m from the end of the corner radius, or from the crossing beyond it; matches `CROSSING_CLEARANCE_MM`), `green-sightline` (the same tool and the planting-height tool: 0.6 to 2.3 m view, 60 cm planting; CityLoom's `SIGHTLINE_MM` is 10 m and differs), `green-clear-path` (corridor tool: 1.8 m minimum, matches `CLEAR_PATH_MM`), `access` (corridor tool: 2.0 to 2.3 m low activity, 2.5 to 3.3 m medium; `ACCESS_LANE_MM` 3 m is in the medium range), `curb-sidewalk` (café-terrasse sheet: 1.8 m local, 2.1 m collector or arterial; CityLoom asks 3.0 m), `crossing` (refuge-island sheet: 1.8 m wide, 2.2 m deep; MTQ says 2 m), `stop-length` (bus-stop sheet: 8 m single-unit, 16 m articulated; `MIN_STOP_MM` 9 m differs), `stop-width` (2.4 m boarding depth matches `BOARDING_MM`), `stop-bike` (raised track at least 1.5 m matches `ISLAND_BIKE_MM`) and `calming-length` (raised-crossing sheet: platform wider than 2.8 m, typically 150 mm high, ramps 12.5 % or 8 % at the least; CityLoom's 3 m plateau agrees, its 100 mm and 1.5 m ramps differ). Where a sheet differs from CityLoom the message says so and the constant is untouched.
