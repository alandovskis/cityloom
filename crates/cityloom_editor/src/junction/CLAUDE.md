# Junction slice (`src/junction/`)

Loaded when working in this folder; the root `CLAUDE.md` has the project-wide rules.

**Read-only (#222).** `JunctionVm::read_only()` is the page's storage's (`Storage::read_only`, a page opened with `view=1`). `apply_with` (so `apply`, `Watch::edit`, `choose_turbo`, `choose_pattern`), `toggle_phase_*`, `undo`, `redo`, `reset` and `load_sample` refuse first and say `read-only-refused` (shell's message; `apply` returns `R::default()`). Behind them `edit` puts back any change to the junction's history (`Junction::same_history`: states and cursor; the selection, region and refusal stay), says `read-only-refused`, returns `R::default()` (nothing is announced as an edit) and never touches the keeper, so nothing is flushed or marked. `link` keeps `view=1`. `region_is_design` is true and `apply_region` takes no region and says nothing read-only (the shell's `choose_region` refuses; see the street's). The page: `page::History` is not drawn; in the inspector `edit_section` (= `section` inside `shared::locked::locked`), `num_view`, `danger_button` and the raw edit sections (lane destinations, crossing mark/remove, transit, remove street, signal tools and patterns) are disabled, `dest_button` and the turns table's buttons get `disabled` and the class `read-only` (they sit outside the locked fieldsets; `editor.css` gives `.turn.read-only` the locked look, so a turn an editable page disables keeps its own); the go-to buttons (`jn-go-*`, path steps, lane picks, whole street, phase cards) stay.

Layout (CLM-32, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-junction-editor-layout-design.md`, step 1): the street page's (`street/CLAUDE.md`, "The editors' layout"): `body.tray-page`; the details `aside.panel.details#details-panel` on the right after `</main>`, its edge tab `#inspector-toggle.edge-tab.right` (`[`), holding `#inspector`; the column `.stage-head` (title card, `#scenario-slot`, `.tools` with `#history`), `.stage-scroll` (the drawing section with `#scroll` and `#plan-key`), and `#notes-slot`, where `mount_page` mounts `view::notes_tray::JunctionNotes` first: the shell's `Tray` with Streets (`t-space`: getting across, turns allowed, where paths meet), Checks and Changes, the status line `#fit` in its handle (`fit-slot`; no `.statusbar`), the fine print and `#title-block` after the panels. The tray starts closed (`NotesAtStart::Closed`). The drawing is laid out in `Frame::in_room(width, room, window)` (`view::frame`): as tall as its column leaves it (the scroller's height less what the card holds around the picture: the part buttons, the plan key, its padding; the card does not grow to fill the column, `.tray-page .plan-drawing { flex: none }`, so the measure holds no slack and the same room always gives the same drawing), between `MIN_ROOM_HEIGHT` (160) and `MAX_HEIGHT` (760), drawn whole and smaller on a short laptop with the tray open; a narrow drawing, or one whose column does not scroll, takes `Frame::fit`'s window formula, which the compare page keeps (`iso_pair`). `iso_svg_framed` draws in a given frame; `iso_svg` is it with `Frame::fit`. The page of a junction that cannot be drawn (`mount_bare_shell`) puts its message in the drawing's place, at the top of `.stage-scroll` (under the title, and scrolled to in a short window), and mounts no tray, so it has no `]` toggle.

Scenarios (#190): `JunctionVm` keeps a `ScenarioState` and is a `ScenarioHost` and `city::binding::ScenarioPage`; its keeper writes to the design shown (`keep_*_in`), its sync reload reads it, and `page::Scenarios` draws `shared::scenarios_view::ScenarioPanel` in `#scenario-slot`, in the head beside the title card, as on the street page (CLM-32: the drawing is as tall as its column leaves it, so a taller head takes room from the drawing, not the status line; see `city/CLAUDE.md`, Scenarios, and `shared/CLAUDE.md`).

Comparison (CLM-9): `compare.html` shows two designs of a junction side by side; `mount_compare_junction` (`mod.rs`) mounts this slice's half. `model/compare.rs` says what differs between two `Junction`s (pure, no wording), `vm/compare.rs` is `JunctionCompareVm` (a `shared::compare::CompareHost`, read-only, nothing written) and `view/compare.rs` draws the two figures per column (`figure.compare-iso`, each `role="img"` with an `aria-label`), over `shared::compare_view::CompareFrame`.

## The details panel (`junction/view/inspector.rs`, `#inspector`)

- One level at a time under a path (`path`, a `nav.panel-path`, `jn-path-label`, the street page's styles): Junction; Junction › a street; Junction › a street › Crossing or Lane n; Junction › Corner, Bus lane or Cycle track. Steps before the last are `.path-step` buttons (`jn-path-up-<i>`) that choose the parent (`Target::None`, `Target::Arm`); the last is `#jn-path-current` (`aria-current="location"`, `tabindex=-1`), which takes the focus after a step or a "go to" button is pressed.
- The Junction level (nothing chosen): the junction's name and `jn-insp-junction-sub` ("4 streets · Traffic signal"), its control (`control_section`, only here: the parts' levels no longer repeat it), its streets as `.btn.jn-go` buttons (`jn-go-<uid>`, compass and name), the signal as one bar of its cycle (`signal_summary`, `.jn-cycle-bar`, each phase's green, yellow and all-red to scale, `aria-hidden`: `jn-sig-summary` "Cycle of 60 s · 2 phases" says it) and `#jn-go-plan`, then the tip `jn-insp-empty`.
- The signal plan is a level of its own, Junction › Signal plan (`jn-path-plan`): `JunctionVm::show_plan` chooses nothing and sets `plan`; `plan_shown` is that flag while nothing is chosen and there is a signal; `select` and `select_relative` clear it, so choosing a part (by pointer or arrow key) or the path's Junction step leaves the plan, and a reload does not keep it. Its signal options are folds (CLM-49, `view::signal_folds`, `inspector::signal_fold` over `fold`): `#jn-fold-plans` above the phases, then `#jn-fold-calls`, `-actuation`, `-bikes`, `-priority`, `-preemption`, each closed at first and not remembered, drawn only where its section has something to list (`signal_folds::shown`, as the section is), its summary the option's name as an `h3.jn-fold-name` (`SignalFold::heading`) carrying the section's heading id (`SignalFold::heading_id`, which the section's `aria-labelledby` names; CLM-50: so the name is reached by heading navigation, open or closed), what is set (`set_line`: "2 crossings on call", "None actuated", for the plans "2 plans, Evening shown") and, when the option has findings, how many (`warnings_line`, `.jn-fold-warn`, the `--warn` colour), so a closed fold hides no problem. The sections inside have no `h3` of their own (the summary's is their heading, once); the lists of calls inside them are `details.jn-fold` too (`#calls-rows`…). e2e: `openSignalFolds` (`e2e/tests/fixtures.ts`) opens the options' folds, by their `jn-fold-` ids only.
- A street's level folds what is rarely set (`fold`, `details.jn-fold`, closed, its name as the summary's `h3.jn-fold-name` and beside it what is set): Transit priority (`#jn-fold-transit`, its heading `#i-h-transit`, `transit_set`: "Bus lane, G2" with `jn-list-comma`, or `jn-fold-none` "None set") and Position (`#jn-fold-position`, its heading `#i-h-position`, `position_set`: "90°, not shifted" / "shifted 0.1 m"; the direction and shift fields). Its signal timing stays in view (it is what a signalised street is set for).
- The ids named with each signal option's section below (`i-h-plans`, `i-h-calls`, `i-h-actuation`, `i-h-bikes`, `i-h-transit-priority`, `i-h-preemption`) are those of its fold's summary heading (CLM-50), which the section's `aria-labelledby` names; the section itself starts at its intro.
- A street's level shows its crossing as one row, `#i-cross-go` ("7.6 m to cross ›", choosing `Target::Crossing`); the crossing's settings are the Crossing level's. A street with no crossing, or a pedestrian one, keeps the old row (mark one, or the note).

## Turbo layouts (`turbo.rs`, `turbo_spiral.rs`, `turbo_ring.rs`)

- Basic, egg, knee, spiral, rotor, in the four Dutch blocks. Figures are placeholders from secondary sources on CROW 2008; unverified ones are marked in doc comments.
- A turbo is a layout of the `ROUNDABOUT` control, not a control: `State.turbo: Option<Turbo>` (layout, block, two main-road arm uids; saved, skipped when `None`, range-checked by `Layout::junction_fits`).
- `turbo.rs`: the layout, not the street, sets entry lanes (an arm cars do not enter gets none) and fixes lane destinations (`set_lane_dest` is refused). Main-road candidates: best opposite pair first. An arm skewed over 20 degrees from its template direction refuses the edit and names the arm; 10 or more fails `turbo-square`.
- `turbo::uses` lane table: main arm first lane left and through, last through and right, middle through only; side arm's outermost lane (right in right-hand traffic, left in left-hand) turns outward only; a lone lane serves every turn.
- `turbo_spiral.rs` is the ring as pure geometry (CETRA 2016 Fig. 3 and 4):
  - Each lane edge is two half circles either side of the translation axis (57.5 degrees from the main road with four arms, 65 with three, mirrored for left-hand traffic), centres shifted along it (island by Δv, other edges by Δu). At the axis a lane's inner edge on one half meets its outer edge on the other within 50 mm (a test pins it for all four blocks).
  - `ring_shape`: one open `RingLane` per half and ring lane (anticlockwise for right-hand traffic), closed island, carriageway and cycle-track outlines, and dividers (each half's R2/R3 arc cut where a street's carriageway meets the ring, `Mouth`; none under 3 m).
  - `spiral_walk`: a movement keeps its lane, steps out a lane at each tip, and in the outermost lane leaves at the tip for the first street after it. `turbo_path` tries a lane further in when the entry's lane cannot reach the street (an egg's or knee's lone lane).
  - A test checks no movement of any layout, block or side crosses a divider (dividers are cut at every street, so an inner lane may leave across the outer lane).
- UNVERIFIED (spec amendment 2026-10-13, J1 to J7): which end of the axis leads (where the tips lie, which arms are side arms; if it flips, main and side swap), where a divider begins and ends, the spiral and rotor blocks (the one-axis block is Egg, Basic and Knee; a third lane is an extension), where a movement leaves at a tip, the egg's and knee's lone lanes.
- `settle_table_and_boxes` drops a turbo when the control, arm count, skew or room for an entry no longer suit it; no bus lane across the island, no queue jump, crossings 6 m from the ring.
- `read_model::layout` widens each entered arm to the layout's lanes (`TURBO_ENTRY_LANE_MM`, 3.5 m, placeholder) and builds the ring on the block. A widened entry past its street's property line is refused as `TurboEntryTooNarrow` (names the arm; a saved turbo with no room is dropped like one past the skew limit); a turbo fitting no block is refused as too big.
- `RingView.lanes` / `.dividers` carry the ring to the 2.5D (dividers open, painted double white line in pieces, `shared::iso::paint`). Its sidewalks stay where the street has them.
- `read_model::choose_turbo` is the one test both `set_turbo` and `turbo_options` use.
- Inspector layout choice: WAI-ARIA radio group (one tab stop; pure `keys::radio_choice` / `radio_tab_stop`); re-choosing the chosen layout is a silent no-op.

## The 2.5D diagram (`junction/view/iso/`, #140)

- `IsoFrame` (plan turned 30 degrees, depth 0.55, height x3) implements `shared::diagram::Projection` (`Pt` = plan x, height, plan y); affine, so `ground_at` is the one matrix for every texture. `build.rs` makes each part's shapes; `scene.rs` (`Scene`) gathers layers into a `Diagram`; `shared::diagram::render` writes it (defs, then one group `iso iso-junction`).
- Layers bottom to top: `Ground`, `Surface` (laid order, places to press among it: order decides a press), `Raised` (far to near), `Markings`, `Objects`, `Signs`, `Outline` (selection). `Hit` stays empty: a place to press lies with what it is on.
- Renderer shapes: `Shape::Path`, `Shape::Elem`, `Shape::Caption`. A textured top on an affine projection is the top plus a `hatch` polygon over it (a fill attribute would lose to the top's CSS fill).
- The 2.5D is the page's only drawing, pressed to select; no flat plan (`plan_svg.rs`), toggle, `V`, grips or `DrawMode`; a value under `cityloom-junction-view` is ignored. `drawing.rs` is the component plus pure `selected_by` / `pressed`.
- Selectable parts carry `data-role` / `data-uid` / `data-lane` (`iso::hit_attrs`): an arm's pieces, a corner's sidewalk, protected track and island, a crossing's island, a roundabout's cycle track and bus lane. Transparent `press_area`s cover an entering lane (`lane-hit`, name as `<title>`), a crossing and a corner with no sidewalk (`iso-hit`).
- All else is `pointer-events: none` (`#drawing .iso-junction`, `#drawing .iso-junction *`), so a standing sidewalk selects its arm. `[data-role]` has `touch-action: manipulation`.
- Target size (WCAG 2.5.8): a place to press under 24 px gets an unpainted stroke to 24 px (`iso::target_growth_px`, `stroke: transparent` in `editor.css`); a corner's place is drawn after the streets so it wins where grown; where neighbours still leave a part less, `#part-previous` / `#part-next` (`jn-part-*`) step through every part like the arrow keys. `e2e/tests/junction-a11y.spec.ts` measures targets at 1440, 1050, 600 and 280 px and keeps a floor.
- Drawing at least `frame::MIN_WIDTH` (240 px): no sideways scroll at 280 px. A zebra and a stop line are pavement paint (`--iso-paint-white` with its dark edge), 3:1 on every material.
- Selection outline: `iso-sel-casing` under `sel-box iso-sel`, over the solids (a cycle track has two), at the height it stands. Nothing is dragged: inspector fields with steppers (`i-h-bearing`, `i-h-corner`, `i-h-setback`) and a key (Shift+Arrow, `+`, `-`).
- `iso_label` names the junction in 2.5D with its streets, and a roundabout, turbo layout or raised table (`jn-iso-label*`).

### Ground marks (`junction/view/iso_marks.rs`, #140 stage 2)

- Pure geometry in ground mm, projected through `IsoFrame::at`. Each line lies on a casing (`iso-casing`; `iso-casing warn` in `--iso-sel-casing` under a warning).
- Drawn: `lane-arrow` on entering lanes (`bad` when only banned turns), `out` where traffic leaves; a roundabout's arrows and "Bus only"; turns of the selected street, lane, crossing or corner (`mv`, banned `no` with a cross, no lane `bad`, `indirect`); `stop-pad`, dwell, run, post; `approach-mark`; a virtual queue jump's loop; a bus gate's line; a dead end's cap; `measure-tag` (Atlas code); `iso-queue`; a failing curbside use's outline and failing corner's curb; a turbo entry's widened road.
- A crossing's length: `dim`, `dim-warn`; overlapping figures move apart by whole lines, the longer crossing keeping its place (`iso_marks::spread_labels`).
- Not drawn: north mark, scale bar, the street's width and compass line, a dead-end arm's fade and hatching, conflict points, a lane's destinations.

## Junction approaches (`street/model/beyond.rs`, `junction/model/beyond.rs`)

- Derived, never saved: no change to `Street`, `State`'s serde or the edits document; `city::edits::VERSION` stays 5.
- `Street::beyond(first_end, frame)` gives an arm `Beyond { boundary_mm, next, zone, taper }`: the first zone boundary going out, the section across it (looking outward), and the `BeyondTaper` (`from_mm`, `to_mm`, `need_mm`, `room_mm`, `merges`, `TaperPiece`s), all from `transition()`; the junction never recomputes a length, host or merge. Distances are from the junction's centre.
- `Arm.beyond: Option<Beyond>` is `#[serde(skip)]` like `Arm.section`, set where `section` is (`City::with_streets_of`, `beyond_from`). Movements, lanes and crossings still read the end zone only.
- `None` for: one zone, no frame, drawn elsewhere (the page's notice), boundary at or past the road's end, same pieces on both sections, or either section wider than `row_mm` (a street failing `fits` draws as before). A section narrower than the street ("left to use") has its beyond: pieces are laid from the left edge, so a mirrored span flips by its own section's total, not `row_mm`.
- `junction/model/beyond.rs` (pure, mm): end-zone pieces stop at the taper's start (a moving piece) or the boundary; one quadrilateral per moving piece at true scale; then the next zone's strips to `ARM_LENGTH_MM` (36 m); two `.approach-mark` lines at the taper's ends (the boundary alone for a jog), dashed, no press, `var(--ink-2)`, `CanvasText` in forced colours, 3:1 on road, sidewalk and planting (`shared/contrast.rs`).
- A taper cut short by its host zone is drawn over the room there is and ends in a jog. A boundary or taper start at or past `ARM_LENGTH_MM` draws nothing new and is neither judged by `approach` nor described in the arm's sentence (`Drawn.shown`). The 2.5D draws the same pieces and counts them in its frame (`content_points`). Arm text: `jn-sel-arm-approach` / `jn-sel-arm-jog`.
- Check `approach` ("Approaches", `jn-check-approach*`; not `Arm.approach` / `APPROACHES`, the transit measures) fails, naming the arm: a steady end zone (to the taper's start, else the boundary) short of its arrows plus a stop line (`ARROW_CLEAR_MM`, 3 m); a host zone shorter than the taper needs; a lane that drops with nothing to merge into.
- Known limits: one boundary per arm (the first); transit measures (`Arm.approach`) are not moved to the boundary; turbo and pedestrian arms draw as before; the zone's start under the mouth is hidden; marks and taper pieces are all that is drawn beyond.
- `e2e/tests/junction.spec.ts` covers the drawing, the check and its failure on a street divided in two.

## Stops at junctions (`street/model/stop_end.rs`, `junction/model/stops.rs`, PR 2 of #70)

- Derived, never saved (no change to `Street`, `State`'s serde, the edits document or `VERSION` 5); `Arm.stops` is read from the street.
- `Street::end_stops(first_end, frame, driving)` reads the street under the side `driving` names (`for_side`, as `Editor::from_street` and `profile_of`; an imported street keeps traffic on the right whatever the region, so `enters`, `lane`, `pad` are as the region's junction reads it; `stops_from` and `City::with_streets` take the region's side).
- It returns stops wholly inside the end zone, nearest first, as `EndStop { index, side, near_mm, far_mm, kind, shelter, name, enters, lane, pad }` in the arm's orientation: mm from the junction's centre, `side` swapped at the last end, `enters` = near-side stop, `lane` / `pad` from the street's own `bus_lane` / `stand` (never recomputed). Empty without a frame, for a frame of another length or anchor, or without stops.
- `Arm.stops` is set beside `Arm.section` / `Arm.beyond` (`stops_from` in `city/model.rs`, cleared with them). The read side draws and judges none on a turbo or pedestrian arm, keeps `near_mm < ARM_LENGTH_MM`, clips to the drawn arm, drops one wholly past it.
- `EndInfo.reach_mm` (`city::model::street_end_infos`, via `Editor::with_ends`): how far the junction reaches along the street (`read_model::arm_reaches`: the crossing's far edge, else the mouth; read on `in_force`). 0 for a pedestrian arm and any turbo arm, so the street page shows no `stop-junction` failure where the junction shows none. `arm_at_end` picks an end's arm: a street returning to its junction has one arm, read at its first end, so its last end has no reach. Computed only for a street with stops, read when the street page opens (stale until reload).
- `STOP_CLEAR_MM` (5 m, UNVERIFIED, same as `CROSSING_CLEARANCE_MM`): how far past the reach a stop's near end must stand (`stop_gap_mm`). Both pages measure the far end from the frame's length, not `length_mm` (may differ within `LENGTH_TOLERANCE_MM`). Closer fails; a negative gap says so (`jn-stop-inside`, `check-detail-stop-junction-inside`), never refused.
- Checks: street page row `stop-junction` (`check-label-stop-junction`, only once an end has a reach); junction `stop-clear` (`jn-check-stop-clear*`) and `stop-lane` (`jn-check-stop-lane*`), each naming stop and arm.
- A lane belongs to a stop only through `junction::model::stops::lane_of`: the section's `travel` piece with direction `toward`, counted from the driver's left. A `bus` piece has no lane or destinations, so a stop in a bus lane is exempt, as is an arm of one entering lane.
- Only an entering stop draws a run (down its lane to the stop line, else the mouth) and is judged for lanes: a lane not going straight on, at a junction with a straight-on movement, fails (`jn-stop-turn-only`); a straight-on lane that also turns toward the kerb fails (`jn-stop-shared-turn`). A transit measure's bus stop (`Arm.stop`, M1/M2) plus a street stop on the same kerb side raise `jn-problem-stop-twice`.
- Drawing: `junction/model/stops.rs::draw` (pure polygons in mm, cut to the drawn arm and `beyond::Drawn.cuts`): `.stop-pad` (+ `.stop-bulb` / `.stop-island`, so kind shows without colour), `.stop-dwell`, `.stop-run`, `.stop-post` (with `.stop-shelter`), `.stop-bad`; on a casing, no press. Arm text `jn-sel-arm-stop*`. Colours and forced-colours rule in `web/editor.css`, contrast pair in `shared/contrast.rs`.
- Known limits: end zone only; a stop straddling the end zone's boundary is not an `EndStop` (judged on the street page only, against the reach; the junction neither draws nor judges it); no routes; turbo and pedestrian arms skip stops; figures are placeholders.
- `e2e/tests/junction.spec.ts` covers a stop too near (drawn, both checks fail, passes 20 m out) and the turning lane (finds the arm with two entering lanes and the side by the street page's "Measured from" end).

## Signal plans (`signal.rs`)
- The cycle has one phase per street with entering lanes, in bearing order, plus which pedestrian crossings and bike movements run with each phase.
- `Junction::set_phase_pattern` gives a phase a ready-made `Pattern` (through only, all movements, protected left then through, pedestrian scramble); `Custom` is what matches none and is never chosen. Offered only where the lanes support it (`signal::patterns_for`, deduplicated), chosen in the inspector by a radio group per phase.
- A scramble serves no movement and is kept as a stored phase with `arm` (written only then, so no version changes).
- `Arm.timing` was added within `city::edits::VERSION` 3 and is saved only once set; version 4 discards version 3 documents, timing included.

## Pedestrian calls (`calls.rs`, #60)
- The first responsive signal behaviour. A crossing is on recall (walked every time its phases run, the default) or on call (walked only when a pedestrian calls it, by a push button or passive detection): `Arm.calls: Option<u32>`, the calls an hour (0 to `MAX_CALLS_PER_HOUR`, 60; `DEFAULT_CALLS_PER_HOUR` 12 when put on call). Saved only once set (`skip_serializing_if`), so a junction with none on call is written as before; a record with one makes the document `city::edits::CALLS_VERSION` (18).
- `Junction::set_on_call` refuses on call unless the control in force is a signal and some phase of `signal::plan` walks the crossing (`calls::refusal`: `NotSignal`, `CrossingOnPedestrian`, `NoCrossing`, `CallNotWalked`); recall is always allowed. `set_call_rate` needs on call (`NotOnCall`) and the bound (`CallRateOutOfRange`). Revisions `jn-rev-call-on` / `-off` / `-rate`.
- Placeholders: `WALK_S` 7, clearance = crossing length (`read_model::crossing_distances` over the view's arms: the drawn `CrossingView.distance_mm`, the one source) at `CLEARANCE_MM_PER_S` (1.0 m/s) rounded up, `MAX_EXTENSION_S` 15.
- `calls::rate_of` is the rate as read (any stored number bounded to 60); never read `Arm.calls` directly.
- `arrivals(uid, rate)`: SplitMix64 (`control::spread`) seeded from the street and the rate, one call in each of `rate` equal slots of the hour (a test pins it). `serve` runs the plan's phases in turn from 0 s on a u64 clock, each at least 1 s, and stops a cycle at its longest (each phase extended by at most `MAX_EXTENSION_S`, `stop_at`) past the last call, whatever still waits then being not served (`settle_table_and_boxes` also clamps every timing field to `TimingField::range` on load and after each edit): a call is served at the start of the first phase at or after it that walks its crossing and can fit walk + clearance with its green lengthened by the shortfall (at most 15 s; yellow and all-red kept; every later phase starts later); a call made while such a phase runs missed its walk and is delayed. Walk + clearance are never shortened.
- Findings (`calls::Finding`, known by `about()`: kind and street): `CannotServe` (no walking phase fits even extended; its calls are not served) and `NotWalked` (on call, but an edit took the walk or the crossing away: kept on call, never dropped). `signal::settle` does not touch `Arm.calls`, but `settle_table_and_boxes` clears it with `Arm.timing` off a signal: `set_control` then wraps its revision in `jn-rev-calls-lost` (with the count), and `Junction::from_city` keeps how many it took off when a record held calls off a signal (`JView.calls_dropped`, shown as `#calls-dropped`, `jn-calls-dropped`, until the first edit; no undo step is added), labelling with `jn-rev-calls-lost` only a state of changes it opens on. A signal suspended by `in_force` (one street of traffic) keeps both: `JView.calls_kept` counts the crossings kept on call, shown as `#calls-kept` (`jn-calls-kept`) under the control's "Only one street here carries traffic".
- Read side: `SignalView.calls` (`CallsView`: each crossing's mode, `walked`, `Totals` with the count and the longest wait, None when no call was served; one row per call, keyed by its `Call`; findings as `Said`, and as `found`). The plan level shows `#signal-calls` after the phases: a box `#call-on-<uid>` per crossing (described by `#call-status-<uid>`: recall, what on call needs, or why it cannot), `#call-rate-<uid>` while on call, the placeholder bounds (`#calls-bounds`), the totals table `#calls-totals` (Crossing, Calls, Served, Delayed, Not served as `td.bad` when any, Green extended, Longest wait or a dash with `.sr-only` words), `#calls-findings`, and every call in the fold `#calls-rows`. Each table scrolls in a focusable region named by its caption, its names' column sticky. The section reads the calls through one `Memo`; a row looks its street's name up by uid (never copied in, so an undo that renames a street keeps rows right).
- Passive detection (`calls::Detection`, CLM-17): a crossing on call detects people by `PushButton` (#60, the default) or `Passive` (kerbside radar, infrared or video). `Arm.detection`, saved only once passive (`skip_serializing_if`), so #60's documents load as push buttons; a record with one makes the document `city::edits::DETECTION_VERSION` (19). Only a crossing on call has one: `settle_table_and_boxes` and `set_on_call` put it back to the push button otherwise; read it through `calls::detection_of`. `Junction::set_detection` refuses passive where `calls::refusal` would refuse on call (no walk is `DetectionNotWalked`) and any detection on recall (`DetectionNeedsCall`); the detection it has is no edit. Revisions `jn-rev-call-passive` / `-push-button`.
- Who leaves (`serve`): the same seeded arrivals; a pedestrian whose walk would start more than `WAIT_LIMIT_S` (30 s, one placeholder for the junction, not editable) after they arrived has left, `Outcome::Cancelled` with a wait of 30 s. A walk everyone waiting for has left is skipped (`Totals.walks_skipped`, from `Run.skipped`) and lengthens no green, so later phases start as if unextended. A push-button call stays placed. Walk and clearance are never shortened. A crossing no phase can serve keeps its calls not served. Push button and passive differ only by the cancelled calls and the walks they skip (pinned where no green is lengthened: a skipped extension moves later phases).
- Its view: `CallCrossingView.detection`; in `#signal-calls`, while on call, a radio group `#call-detect-<uid>` (named by `#call-detect-h-<uid>`, described by `#call-detect-help-<uid>`, what the chosen detection does; radios `#call-detect-<uid>-push-button` / `-passive`, kept while the crossing stays on call so a radio keeps the focus; locked read-only with the rest), the waiting limit in `#calls-bounds`, `#calls-totals` columns Cancelled and Walks skipped, and `jn-calls-outcome-cancelled` in `#calls-rows`. `text::calls_text` adds `jn-calls-said-passive` (cancelled, skipped) for a passive crossing. Not modelled: on-crossing detection (extending clearance for slow walkers), induction loops, detector accuracy.
- Announcements: `JunctionVm::set_on_call` / `set_call_rate` / `set_detection` say the edit, the crossing's totals (`text::calls_text`, `jn-calls-said` or `-no-wait`, from the same `Totals`) and any new finding; an unchanged rate or detection says nothing (it relies on `set_call_rate` / `set_detection` returning true without adding a revision, which `call_edit` checks). Every other edit, undo, redo, reset, region change and sync reload (`on_synced`, which also says the crossings it took off call) also says a finding that arose (compared by `Finding::about`, so a rename is no new finding) and, when it suspends the signal, the crossings kept on call.

## The signal controller (`control.rs`, CLM-12)
- One deterministic loop over the hour for every responsive policy: `control::run(plan, rules, demands, turns)` runs the phases in turn on a u64 clock and returns every `Turn` (start, green start and end, end, `base_s`, `held_s`, `cut_s`, `prolonged_s`, `preempted_s`, `kept`, skipped; `Turn::given_s` the green before any demand cut, held or interrupted it). `control::hour(s, plan, distances, rides)` is the one entry (`rides` from `read_model::ride_lengths`): rules from `actuation::rules`, demands `calls::Walks`, `actuation::Vehicles`, the bicycle calls (`bicycles::riders`) the buses (`transit::Buses`) and the emergency calls (`preemption::Emergencies`), giving `Hour { calls, actuation, bicycles, transit, preemption }`; `calls::summary` is its `.calls`, and `read_model::signal_view` runs it once for both views.
- A phase's `Rule`: `Fixed` (runs every turn at its timing's green) or `Actuated { bounds, floor_s }` (skipped, taking no time, when no demand `calls` it; when a whole round is skipped the signal rests until the next arrival, `next_after`). Green: fixed = timing's green; actuated = `extend` from the floor, within the maximum; then lengthened to the longest `hold` (an actuated phase's arrivals still extend past a hold, within the maximum); then `cut` short as far as a demand asks (never below the hold) and `prolong`ed as far as one asks. Transit lead, yellow and all-red are the timing's: never shortened.
- A policy is a `Demand` (`calls`, `extend`, `hold`, `cut`, `prolong`, `ran`, `pending`, `next_after`, `last`, `most_extension`, `most_prolong`, `longest_hold`; for pre-emption `jump`, `interrupt`, `clear_by`, `keeps`, `kind`, `waiting`, `counts`, `census`, `out_of_turn`), its rules in its own file. Bicycle calls (CLM-13) are the third, buses (CLM-14) the fourth, emergency calls (CLM-15) the fifth, above the others through `jump` / `interrupt` / `clear_by`; later ones plug in as further demands or rules; the loop itself knows none of them. Time-of-day plans (CLM-16) are no demand: the hour runs one plan's settings (`plans::applied`). Arrivals of every demand come from `control::spread` on a seed stream of its own.
- With a phase actuated the run covers the whole hour (its skips are counted to the end, even with nobody coming); a plan of fixed phases stops once nobody waits or is to come, as before.
- `stop_at`: the last arrival (or the hour) plus a cycle at its longest (fixed: length + the most any demand extends, `MAX_EXTENSION_S`; actuated: its maximum, floor or longest hold); a guard, cutting off nobody. `MAX_TURNS` counts phases run, not skipped.
- With no street actuated every rule is `Fixed` and the calls are served exactly as #60 and CLM-17 pinned them (their tests run unchanged).

## Vehicle actuation (`actuation.rs`, CLM-12)
- A street with a phase of its own (`own_phase`: `Phase.uid` and moves) is on recall (default) or actuated: `Arm.actuation: Option<Actuation>` (`min_green_s`, `gap_s`, `max_green_s`, `vehicles` an hour), saved only once set; a record with one makes the document `city::edits::ACTUATION_VERSION` (20). Read it through `actuation_of` (each `Field` clamped to its range, max raised to min); `settle_table_and_boxes` settles it too, and clears it off a signal like the timing (the control's revision says the timing went).
- Placeholders: defaults 7 s / 3 s / 45 s / 300 an hour; ranges 5–30 s, 1–10 s, 10–120 s, 0–1800 an hour; steppers 1 s, 10 vehicles. `Junction::set_actuated` refuses where `actuation::refusal` does (`NotSignal`, `ActuationNoPhase`) and the mode it has (`ActuationAlready`); `set_actuation` needs actuated (`NotActuated`), the range (`ActuationOutOfRange`) and min ≤ max (`MinAboveMax`); a value it has is no edit. Revisions `jn-rev-actuated-on` / `-off`, `jn-rev-actuation` (`-vehicles`).
- The rule (doc comment at the top of `actuation.rs`): the phase of an actuated street is actuated; a vehicle waits for the next phase that moves its approach; an actuated phase nobody calls (no vehicle, no pedestrian call it would serve; a crossing on recall does not call) is skipped; once green it runs its floor (min green, or a recall crossing's walk + clearance when longer), each arrival holds it `gap_s` more (timed even during the floor), up to the maximum (gap-out = ended early, max-out). A served walk + clearance holds it past both. On-call crossings walked in an actuated phase can always be served there (`calls::Walks.serving`, `cannot_serve`).
- `arrivals(uid, rate)`: `control::spread` seeded with `VEHICLE_STREAM` ("VEHICLES") xor the street and rate, so never the calls of the same street (pinned). Not modelled: queue discharge (a queue goes as the green starts and holds no longer than the floor), lanes, detector placement, resting in green.
- Summary: `PhaseTotals` per actuated phase (served, skipped, extended past the floor and by how much in all, ended early before the maximum) and `ApproachTotals` per actuated street with a phase (vehicles, served, not served, average and longest wait or None). Findings (`about()` is `(kind, arm, crossing)`): `NoPhase` (kept actuated, its vehicles call nothing) and `WalkPastMax` (a crossing walked in an actuated phase needs more than its maximum: the walk holds the green past it).
- Its view: `SignalView.actuation` (`ActuationView`: streets with a phase of their own or actuated, their settings, phase and approach totals, findings as `Said` and `found`). In the plan level after `#signal-calls`, `#signal-actuation` (`i-h-actuation`): `#actuation-bounds`; per street a group (`jn-act-group`) with the radio group `#act-mode-<uid>` (named by `#act-mode-h-<uid>`, described by `#act-status-<uid>`: recall, trigger and bounds, the floor when a walk raises it, or why it cannot; radios `-recall` / `-actuated`), while actuated the fields `#act-min|gap|max|rate-<uid>` (`act_field`: the `.stepper` markup, buttons `#act-<f>-<uid>-less|more`, described by `#act-<f>-range-<uid>`) and `#act-effect-<uid>`; `#actuation-none`; tables `#actuation-phases` (a phase's name looked up by its place) and `#actuation-approaches` (not served `td.bad`, no wait a dash with `.sr-only` words), each a focusable region named by its caption; `#actuation-findings`. The list is locked read-only.
- Announcements: `JunctionVm::set_actuated` / `set_actuation` go through `policy_edit` (as the calls' `call_edit`): the edit, `text::actuation_text` (`jn-act-said` or `-no-wait`) and any new finding of either policy (`findings_note` compares the calls' and the actuation's `about()`).

## Bicycle detection (`bicycles.rs`, CLM-13)
- A street's bike movement (its cyclists, going where its traffic goes; `Phase.rides` holds the street's uid) is on recall (default, runs with every phase that lets it go) or on detection: `Arm.cyclists: Option<u32>`, the cyclists an hour, saved only once set; a record with one makes the document `city::edits::BICYCLE_VERSION` (21). Read it through `bicycles::rate_of` (bounded to 120). Off a signal it goes with the timing (`settle_table_and_boxes`; `set_control` says `jn-rev-control-timing`).
- `Junction::set_bike_detection` refuses where `bicycles::refusal` does (`NotSignal`, `BikeNotRidden`: no phase of `signal::plan` runs it, e.g. no bike lane) and the mode it has (`BikeDetectionAlready`); `set_cyclists` needs detection (`NotOnBikeDetection`) and 0–120 (`CyclistsOutOfRange`); a rate it has is no edit. Revisions `jn-rev-bike-detection-on` / `-off`, `jn-rev-cyclists`.
- Placeholders: 30 an hour when put on detection, 0–120; `MIN_GREEN_S` 5; clearance = ride length at `SPEED_MM_PER_S` (4 m/s) rounded up; `MAX_EXTENSION_S` is the calls' 15 s. Ride length: `read_model::ride_lengths`, the street's `mouth_at` to the farthest mouth its lanes go to (every other street's when it sends none): the read model has no bike path of its own.
- The rule (doc comment at the top of `bicycles.rs`) is the pedestrian calls' (push button): the demand is `calls::Walks::over(.., bicycles::rides_it)` and the finding `calls::unserved`. Served at the start of the next phase running the movement that fits `need_s` (fixed: green + 15 s; actuated: always, it holds), delayed when they came while one ran; a fixed green is lengthened by the shortfall. With a walk in the same phase both are holds, so the longer is kept and neither is shortened; a cyclist calls an actuated phase, and a served need holds its green past gap-out and maximum. On recall nothing changes (no floor for an actuated phase). `arrivals`: `control::spread` on `BIKE_STREAM` ("BICYCLES"), pinned.
- Summary: `Ride` per call (served, delayed, not served; wait; extended by), `Totals` per movement (calls, served, delayed, not served, extended and by how much, longest wait), findings (`about()` kinds 20, 21, 22): `CannotServe` (no phase fits even extended; not served), `NotRidden` (kept on detection, no phase runs it) and `PastMax` (an actuated phase running it has a maximum below its need: the green is held past it, as actuation's `WalkPastMax`; `jn-bikes-finding-past-max`).
- Its view: `SignalView.bicycles` (`BicyclesView`: movements run by the plan or on detection, `BikeMovementView` with `label` and `to` as `Said`, rate, `ridden`, length, clearance, need, totals; `rows` reuse the calls' outcome words). In the plan level after `#signal-actuation`, `#signal-bikes` (`i-h-bikes`): `#bikes-bounds`; per movement a group `.bike-movement` named "Bikes from A to B" with the radio group `#bike-mode-<uid>` (named by `#bike-mode-h-<uid>`, described by `#bike-status-<uid>`: recall, trigger and bound, or why not; radios `-recall` / `-detection`), while on detection `#bike-rate-<uid>` (described by `#bike-range-<uid>`) and `#bike-effect-<uid>`; `#bikes-none`; `#bikes-totals` (Bike movement, Calls, Served, Delayed, Extended "n (s)", Longest wait) in a focusable region named by its caption; `#bikes-findings`; every call in the fold `#bikes-rows`. Locked read-only.
- Announcements: `JunctionVm::set_bike_detection` / `set_cyclists` through `policy_edit`: the edit, `text::bikes_text` (`jn-bikes-said` or `-no-wait`) and any new finding (`findings_note` compares the calls', the actuation's and the bicycles' `about()`).

## Transit signal priority (`transit.rs`, CLM-14)
- A street with transit (`transit::has_transit`: `bus_lane`, `approach` ≠ none (queue jump, bus gate), `stop` ≠ none (M1/M2), `filter`, or `Arm.stops` non-empty) and a phase of its own (`actuation::own_phase`) gives its buses no priority (default), an early green, a green extension or both: `Arm.transit_priority: Option<Priority { mode, buses }>`, saved only once set; a record with one makes the document `city::edits::TRANSIT_VERSION` (22). Read it through `transit::priority_of` (buses bounded to 60). Off a signal it goes with the timing.
- `Junction::set_transit_priority(uid, Option<Mode>)` refuses where `transit::refusal` does (`NotSignal`, `PriorityNoTransit`, `PriorityNoPhase`); none is always allowed; the mode it has is no edit; a change of mode keeps the rate. `set_buses` needs priority (`NotOnPriority`) and 0–60 (`BusesOutOfRange`). Revisions `jn-rev-priority-early-green` / `-extension` / `-both` / `-off`, `jn-rev-buses`.
- Placeholders: `EARLY_GREEN_MAX_S` 10, `EXTENSION_MAX_S` 10 (the user's), 12 buses an hour when turned on, 0–60. `arrivals`: `control::spread` on `BUS_STREAM` ("BUSES"), pinned.
- The rule (doc comment at the top of `transit.rs`): a bus in its street's green (from the phase's start, the transit lead included) goes on arrival. Early green: a bus waiting during red, or coming before the running green ends, cuts the greens of the phases before its own (`Demand::cut`), at most 10 s in all for that green, never ending one before the bus came, never below the rule's minimum (`GREEN_MIN_S` fixed, min green actuated), what the phase keeps (`transit::kept`: actuated floor, walks of crossings on recall, bike movements on recall) or what a demand holds; seconds kept for a walk or bike clearance are `Finding::ClearanceHeld` (never silent). A phase whose own street's bus is due (before its green ends, or within 10 s after when that street may be given an extension) is never cut; with several streets waiting the one whose phase comes next asks. Extension (`Demand::prolong`): a bus within 10 s after its green would end holds it to the second after it came. No priority for the green after one given priority (`greens` / `given`; both in one green count as one). A bus calls its actuated phase. The transit lead stays a fixed lead every cycle; priority is on demand on top.
- Delay moved (`Moved { from, to, seconds }`): seconds cut from another approach's green, and each extension's seconds once for each other approach whose next green runs before the street's own runs again (a skipped actuated phase bears none). Summary: `Bus` per bus (`OnArrival`, `EarlyGreen`, `Extended`, `Delayed`, `NotServed`; wait, early, extended), `Totals` per street (buses, the five outcomes, longest wait, `moved_s`), findings `NoTransit`, `NoPhase` (kept, gives nothing), `ClearanceHeld { arm, phase, times, held_s }` (`about()` kinds 30, 31, 32).
- Its view: `SignalView.transit` (`PriorityView`: streets with transit or priority, `PriorityStreetView` with priority, `has_transit`, `has_phase`, totals, `moved` as a `Said` list; rows `BusRowView`). In the plan level after `#signal-bikes`, `#signal-transit` (`i-h-transit-priority`), shown while some street has transit or priority (hidden otherwise, as `#signal-actuation` and `#signal-bikes`): `#transit-bounds`; per street a group `.transit-street` ("Buses on A") with the radio group `#transit-mode-<uid>` (named by `#transit-mode-h-<uid>`, described by `#transit-status-<uid>`: none, trigger and bounds, or why not; radios `-off` / `-early-green` / `-extension` / `-both`), while on `#transit-rate-<uid>` (described by `#transit-range-<uid>`), `#transit-effect-<uid>` and `#transit-moved-<uid>`; `#transit-none` (no street gives priority); `#transit-totals` (Street, Buses, On arrival, Early green, Extended, Delayed, Longest wait, Delay moved to others) in a focusable region named by its caption; `#transit-findings`; every bus (Bus, Arrived, Outcome, Wait) in the fold `#transit-rows`. Locked read-only.
- Announcements: `JunctionVm::set_transit_priority` / `set_buses` through `policy_edit`: the edit, `text::transit_text` (`jn-transit-said` or `-no-wait`) and any new finding (`findings_note` includes the transit's `about()`).

## Emergency vehicle pre-emption (`preemption.rs`, CLM-15)
- A street with a phase of its own (`actuation::own_phase`) has pre-emption off (default) or on: `Arm.preemption: Option<u32>`, its emergency calls an hour, saved only once set; a record with one makes the document `city::edits::PREEMPTION_VERSION` (23). Read it through `preemption::rate_of` (bounded to 12). Off a signal it goes with the timing.
- `Junction::set_preemption(uid, bool)` refuses on where `preemption::refusal` does (`NotSignal`, `PreemptionNoPhase`); off always allowed; the setting it has is no edit. `set_emergency_calls` needs it on (`NotOnPreemption`) and 0–12 (`EmergencyCallsOutOfRange`). Revisions `jn-rev-preemption-on` / `-off`, `jn-rev-emergency-calls`.
- Placeholders: 2 calls an hour when turned on, 0–12, `DWELL_S` 20. `arrivals`: `control::spread` on `EMERGENCY_STREAM` ("PREEMPT"), pinned.
- The rule (doc comment at the top of `preemption.rs`), on the loop's own hooks (`Demand::jump`, `interrupt`, `clear_by`; `Turn.preempted_s`): a call ends the green running at once, except that a walk (or bicycle minimum green) still showing is cut and its clearance then runs in full, and a clearance under way runs out (on call: `Walks::clear_by`; on recall: `preemption::recall`; `calls::clear_at`); yellow and all-red always in full; no other demand cuts or prolongs an interrupted green (a call during a transit extension ends it there; one after a bus's early green already ended it interrupts nothing; `control::settle`), and none cuts or interrupts a green running for a call (`Demand::keeps`: its street's phase with the first call waiting or coming before the green ends). The street's phase then runs out of turn and holds its green `DWELL_S` from its start (or from the call, when already green: time to green 0; a further call of the street during it holds it `DWELL_S` past that call). Return: the plan goes on with the phase after the one interrupted (the loop's planned phase is kept while a phase runs out of turn); a phase that was next anyway runs once. Calls are served in order of arrival; one during another street's pre-emption waits for its green to end. It calls an actuated phase.
- Counting (`Demand::kind`, `waiting`, `census`): each call's pre-empted (users of the interrupted phase waiting when the call came, before the turn is told) and delayed (users waiting for any phase but its own at the call's second: before the turn is told when the call came by its start, a rest gap say, else after it; zero when the call changed nothing: no interruption, no jump, no green held past its rule's), by `control::Kind` (pedestrian, bicycle, bus, vehicle).
- Summary: `Call` per call (to green, `interrupted`, `preempted`, `delayed` as `Counts`), `Totals` per street (calls, served, longest to green, `entry_s`, preempted and delayed summed), finding `NoPhase` (`about()` kind 40). `entry_s`: over the phases it can interrupt, the longest pedestrian or bicycle clearance + yellow + all-red, + its own transit lead.
- Its view: `SignalView.preemption` (`PreemptionView`: streets with a phase or pre-emption, `PreemptStreetView` with rate, `has_phase`, `entry_s`, totals, `preempted` / `delayed` as `Said` lists; rows `EmergencyRowView`). In the plan level after `#signal-transit`, `#signal-preemption` (`i-h-preemption`), shown while some street is listed: intro (highest priority), `#preempt-bounds`; per street a group `.preempt-street` ("Emergency vehicles on A") with the radio group `#preempt-mode-<uid>` (named by `#preempt-mode-h-<uid>`, described by `#preempt-status-<uid>`: off, trigger + priority + clearance + safe entry + dwell, or why not; radios `-off` / `-on`), while on `#preempt-rate-<uid>` (described by `#preempt-range-<uid>`) and `#preempt-effect-<uid>`; `#preempt-none`; `#preempt-totals` (Street, Calls, Longest time to green, Pre-empted, Delayed) in a focusable region named by its caption; `#preempt-findings`; every call (Call, Arrived, Time to green, Pre-empted, Delayed) in the fold `#preempt-rows`. Locked read-only.
- Announcements: `JunctionVm::set_preemption` / `set_emergency_calls` through `policy_edit`: the edit, `text::preempt_text` (`jn-preempt-said` or `-no-call`) and any new finding.

## Time-of-day plans (`plans.rs`, CLM-16)
- The default plan is the arms' own settings (`timing`, `calls`, `detection`, `actuation`, `cyclists`, `transit_priority`, `preemption`): unchanged code reads it. `State.plans` holds up to 7 named `TimingPlan { id, name, streets }` (`MAX_PLANS` 8 with the default, names ≤ 40 chars, placeholders), each a full copy of every street's `Settings` (same fields and serde as `Arm`'s; a street it lacks runs as the default). Ids are stable (max + 1; the default is 0, never stored). Phases, order and patterns are the junction's, shared.
- `plans::applied(s, id)` is the junction as plan `id` sees it; `signal::plan`, `control::hour`, the checks and the read model run on it unchanged. `Junction.viewing` (not history, as `selected`; `view_plan`, `fix_viewing` falls back to the default when the plan goes) is the plan shown: `Junction::view` reads `applied(current, viewing)`, so the signal view, checks, totals and findings are its. Every timing and policy edit (`set_timing`, `step_timing`, `set_on_call`, `set_detection`, `set_call_rate`, `set_actuated`, `set_actuation`, `set_bike_detection`, `set_cyclists`, `set_transit_priority`, `set_buses`, `set_preemption`, `set_emergency_calls`) is a wrapper `in_plan(|j| j.<name>_here(..))`: for a named plan the `_here` edit runs with all its checks on a scratch junction of the applied state, and the settings it leaves are kept as the plan's in one revision `jn-rev-in-plan` ("…, in plan “Night”"); a setting it has is no edit. So the existing controls edit the plan shown; nothing is duplicated.
- `State.schedule` (`Schedule { weekday, saturday, sunday }`: `Period { start_min, plan }` after the first): `periods(day)` always starts at 00:00 (the default unless a period at 0 says otherwise), each runs to the next start, the last to midnight; `plan_at`, `plan_in_week` (Monday 00:00 = 0). `Schedule::settle` keeps starts distinct, sorted, < 1440, plans known (else the default), drops a `{0, default}`, at most `MAX_PERIODS` (12, the first included): no gap or overlap can be stored (a seeded walk of random edits checks every minute of the week). No transition period: a change of plan takes effect at the end of the running cycle (said in the plan view; the hour runs one plan).
- Edits: `add_plan` (copies the default), `rename_plan`, `remove_plan` (its periods then run the default; `jn-rev-plan-remove` says how many), `add_period`, `remove_period`, `set_period_plan`, `set_period_start`. Refusals `PlanNameEmpty`, `PlanNameTooLong`, `PlanNameTaken` (any case), `TooManyPlans`, `DefaultPlanKept`, `DefaultPlanNamed`, `PeriodOutOfDay` (start 1..1439), `PeriodTaken`, `TooManyPeriods`, `FirstPeriodKept`, `NotSignal`. `settle_table_and_boxes` runs `settle_settings` on the arms and (`plans::settle`) on every plan's settings; off a signal plans and schedule go with the timing (`set_control` says `jn-rev-control-timing`). Saved only once there is a plan or a schedule (`skip_serializing_if`); a record with either makes the document `city::edits::PLANS_VERSION` (24).
- Read side: `JView.plans: Option<PlansView>` (with a signal): `viewing`, `PlanView { id, name, own_name, is_default, cycle_s, findings }` (each plan checked on its own: "Time to cross" and "Signal cycle" failures, `walk_short` / `cycle_long`, plus its policies' findings over its own hour, `plan_findings`), `DayView { day, periods: PeriodView { start_min, end_min, plan } }`, `PlansView::runs_at(day, minute)`.
- The plan level opens on `#signal-plans` (`i-h-plans`): `#plan-shown` (a select, described by `#plan-shown-help`; never locked), `#plans-list` (`#plan-<id>`, `#plan-default-mark`, `#plan-summary-<id>` cycle and finding count, `#plan-findings-<id>`, `#plan-name-<id>` renames on change, `#plan-remove-<id>`), `#plan-new-name` + `#plan-add` (Enter adds; `JunctionVm::add_plan` shows the new plan), `#plans-bounds`, a table a day type `#schedule-<day>` (caption `schedule-<day>-caption`, a focusable region; rows keyed by start, plan and end, ids by start: `#period-start-<day>-<min>` time field (a start changed sends the focus to the period's new start field, `focus_after_swap`), first row fixed 00:00 "Starts the day", end, `#period-plan-<day>-<min>`, `#period-remove-<day>-<min>`) with `.period-add` (`#period-new-start-<day>`, `#period-new-plan-<day>`, `#period-add-<day>`), `#runs-at-day` / `#runs-at-time` / `#runs-at` (`role=status`), `#plans-transition`. `#plan-viewing-note` in a street's timing and the junction's cycle while a named plan is shown. Read-only: the chooser and the runs-at question stay usable (`view_plan` changes no history); the rest is locked.
- Announcements: `JunctionVm::view_plan` says `jn-plans-shown-said` (not when already shown); `add_plan` says the edit then the plan shown; other plan and schedule edits go through `apply` (label or refusal). A sync reload or scenario switch keeps the plan shown where it still exists.
