# Map slice (`src/map/`)

Loaded when working in this folder; the root `CLAUDE.md` has the project-wide rules.

## Map page and overlay
- Exception to the root's SVG rule: MapLibre map on a plain ground, no SVG. `svg.rs` and `camera.rs` remain only for the city drawn behind the About page's card (`map/view/home.rs`: `HeroMap`, `HeroFacts`, `AboutTitle`; `mount_about`, composed by `lib.rs`). The drawing is `inert` and `aria-hidden`, so its place links can be neither pressed nor reached. The About page (`web/about.html`, `about.css` over `home.css`; messages `map-about-*` and `map-home-*`) links to the projects, never to a map: it is in no project.
- `overlay.rs` makes GeoJSON places (`hot` ids `s-<uid>` / `j-<uid>`; streets carry `class`); `style.rs` holds the layers (dotted white `places-pedestrian`; `zone-ticks` via `overlay::zone_ticks`, `style::zone_tick_layers`, `Mapper::set_zone_ticks`; rules in the root's Street zones bullet). `city::projection` places the layout on the earth.
- Streets join as one surface: lines have square ends and `overlay::joints` joins them at each node two streets or more end at. Where two meet, a disc (`joint_m`, a Point) at the end of the narrower and as wide as it, so streets at an angle join round and a wide street going on narrower does not bulge; where three or more meet, a shape (a Polygon, `Join::Middle`, `overlay::middle`): the ground they share, the union of pieces that each hold the node, traced from it into one simple ring. Between two streets next to each other 10 to 170 degrees apart, the ground up to where their kerbs meet ahead, no further than twice the narrower's half-width and less towards 10 or 170 degrees (no step there, no long taper between near-straight streets of different widths); along each street, as far as that ground reaches; across the outside of a bend or between near-straight streets, the triangle to their ends' corners. Streets leaving within half a degree are one. Two streets crossing make the square where their kerbs cross, a side street off a through street the box of the side street across it, every street's end corners are under it, and nothing reaches past a kerb but between neighbours and across the outside of a bend; the tests check every shape for this, and for being one simple ring round the node, over 2,000 random meetings too (half on compass bearings). `None` where no two streets' kerbs meet (the disc then). A lone end (dead end, map edge) stays square, so a short wide stub is no disc. A disc is its street's (`hot`, status, conflict), drawn by `places-joint` under the streets' lines; a shape not at a junction is its own (`m-<node>`, plain colour, never lit or pressed, editable only where every street that meets there is, `overlay::meetings` (made once for each city view, `MapVm::meetings_of`) and `editable::with_meetings`, so it is never solid under a muted street), drawn by `places-middle` under the lines too, so it fills only the gaps between their ends and every street keeps its own colour and highlight to its end. At a junction the join is the junction's ground (`ground`, the junction's `hot` and status), drawn over the streets' ends, so it lights and opens with the junction: a shape by `places-ground`, a disc (only two of its streets in the place) by `places-junction-ground`. `places-joint-casing` draws every disc's casing and `places-middle-casing` every shape's outline (`CASING_PX`, half of it outside) under every line; none has a stroke over the streets, and none is drawn where its place is muted (a translucent fill over translucent lines shows lighter). A junction is a 5 px marker (`JUNCTION_MARK_PX`, `["has", "radius_m"]`, so joints are never taken for junctions). It is pressed, first in `hotAt` and under the pointer alone, on `places-ground` and on the unseen `places-junction-hit`, at least 12 px round (WCAG 2.5.8) and `radius_m` (`overlay::target_m`): its own radius or its disc ground's, but nothing where its ground is a shape, so the wide street past a narrow one's corners is pressed as the street. A Point in `places` is a junction only with `radius_m`.
- The view-model reaches MapLibre only through the `Mapper` port.
- `editable.rs`: in the open view (`MapEvent::View`, what the panels leave open) editable = a street wholly in it, a street too big for it while it crosses it, a junction whose point is in it. Sent by `Mapper::set_editable` (feature-state `editable`; `style.rs` mutes the rest). A press on a muted place opens nothing and says `map-not-editable`.
- `MapEvent::Press` and `MapEvent::Vertex` (corner dragged) reach Rust, which decides what a press means.
- A press on a place shows it in the details (`MapVm::pick`, `picked_row`, `picked_failing`; announced `map-picked`); a second press on the place shown, or `#map-open-place`, opens its editor; a press on nothing goes back to the city. The details are levels under a path (`map/view/details.rs`: `MapPath` in `#map-path-slot`, `PlacePane` in `#map-place-slot`): `#map-city` (no tabs: whether it works and the places that do not, the goal, a row per network check, rows to the area rules, the selected streets and the changes, and what a press does, `map-city-hint`), `#map-place` (name, kind, the checks it fails, Open, and for a street Add to / Drop from the selection), `#map-areas` (the area rules), `#map-check` (one network check and its findings), `#map-selection` (the selected streets and the change to try on them) or `#map-changes` (the places changed; `ChangesRow`, `#map-go-changes`, `MapVm::show_changes` / `changes_summary`), one shown at a time (`MapVm::level`). A level other than the city, newly chosen, opens the details if they are closed (`details::reveal`, which presses the shell's toggle and then `MapVm::say_again`). The path's styles (`.panel-path`, `.path-step`, `.path-here`) are in `app.css`, shared with the editors.
- Area rules: `areas.rs` (`AreasVm`, held by `MapVm`) + `areas_view.rs`, a level of the details (`#map-areas`, `MapLevel::Areas`), reached from the city's row (`AreasRow`, `#map-go-areas`, `MapVm::areas_summary`) or by pressing an area on the map; a press on nothing there lets the area go and stays. An outline being drawn or named always shows this level and the path's City is no step back until it is finished or cancelled (`can_leave_areas`); if the details are closed the notes toggle is pressed (`reveal`). The map has no left panel, no `[` key and gives no room to one (`body.map-page`, `--lw: 0`). Uses `restriction::draw`; the map owns the restriction UI.
- Status line and checks lead count area-rule breakers only when no design check fails.
- No kept roads for the area: no places, no map, page says so (`NO_ROADS`). A map that cannot start (no WebGL) says `map-canvas-missing`.
- The city is read on open, on `pageshow` and when another tab writes edits; edits made on a street/junction page show on next load (known limit).

## Read-only (#222)

`MapVm::read_only()` and `AreasVm::read_only()` are the page's storage's (`Storage::read_only`, `view=1`). `review_corridor`, `apply_corridor`, `undo_corridor` and, in `AreasVm`, `start`, `start_redraw`, `save`, `change` (`rename`, `set_rule`), `delete`, `undo_delete` and a corner drop (`drag_vertex` with `done`; its moves show nothing; `sync` sends the selected area without its corner handles, `overlay::areas(.., handles: false)`, so a press there pans) refuse and say `read-only-refused` (shell's message); `can_declare` is false. Selection, search, the camera, the levels, the checks and the report go on. `street_href`/`junction_href`/`href_of` take `read_only`, so every place the map opens opens read-only (`project_id::link_in`). The page: Declare, the deleted area's Undo, the corridor change and its undo are not drawn; the selected area's form (rule, name, Redraw, Delete) is inside `shared::locked::locked`.

## Selected streets (`map/vm/selection.rs`, `selection_view.rs`; #66)
- `SelectionVm` (held by `MapVm::selection()`) is a page-long list of street uids in the order added, not kept. A Shift-click on the map (`web/mapcanvas.js` emits `MapEvent::Toggle`, not `Press`) or Shift+Enter on a search result (`MapVm::toggle_chosen`) adds a street or drops it; a junction or blank map says `sel-not-a-street`, a street not wholly in view says `map-not-editable` (a selected one can always be dropped).
- `Mapper::set_selected` sends the `s-<uid>` ids; the adapter sets feature-state `selected` on the `places` source (re-applied with the places) and `style.rs`'s `places-selected` draws a dashed dark-blue line down those streets (a pattern, not colour alone).
- A change to the selection (`map/model/corridor.rs`, the panel's `corridor` group): `Op` = a street template or Make pedestrian; `plan` tries it on a copy of each street's editor (`Editor::apply_template` / `make_pedestrian`), so a street that cannot says why (`Said`) and nothing is written; `apply` re-plans against the stored city and writes each street that takes it through `CityStore::write_street`, leaving the rest untouched. `MapVm` holds `Stage` (`Idle` → `Reviewing(Plan)` → `Done(Report)`); changing the selection or the picked change drops a review; only a review can be applied. A change that changed a street is kept as an `Undo` (each street as it was and as it was left) for the page's life: the `#corridor-undo` button (shown whenever there is one, selection or not) puts every street back as one step, and refuses (and goes), naming how many, when any street is no longer as the change left it (edited on its own page, by sync or another tab): nothing is restored then. A new change replaces it; it is not kept across a reload.
- The Selected streets (`#selection-panel`) are a level of the details (`#map-selection`, `MapLevel::Selection`): a street added (`toggle_selected`) shows it, and the city's row (`SelectionRow`, `#map-go-selection`, `MapVm::selection_summary`) leads there; emptied, it stays (its lead and Undo are there). It lists them with a remove button each (`aria-label` names the street) and a clear button; every change is announced with the count (`sel-*`). A selection changes nothing yet.

## Curbside uses and transit stops on the map
- `Street::map_uses` (`street/model/map_uses.rs`) turns `Street.curb` / `Street.stops` into `MapUse` / `MapStop` (side, along range in mm from node `a` along `shape_mm`, lateral `lo_mm`/`hi_mm`). `city::model` puts them in `EdgeView.curb_mm` / `.stops_mm` under the same gate as `zone_bands` (frame is the street's own end, length within `LENGTH_TOLERANCE_MM`). A street with neither draws and counts as before.
- Lateral offset is positive to the right looking from `a` to `b` (layout mm, x east, y south; right normal of `(tx, ty)` is `(-ty, tx)`), `Side::Left` negative, measured from `-row_mm/2` like `PieceView.offset_mm`, so a use sits on the drawn pavement, also in a zone narrower than the row.
- A use/stop across a zone boundary is cut there, each part taking its zone's section. A part with no curbside piece (or a stop whose section lacks what its kind needs) draws nothing, as in the 2.5D.
- `overlay::curbside` / `overlay::stops`: thin polygon along `shape_mm` pushed to the two edges along the averaged normal, capped at 2x at a bend; a stop is a `platform` polygon plus a `dot` point carrying its `name`.
- Layers: `curbside-casing`/`-fill`/`-line` from zoom 16, `stops-platform`/`stops-dot` from 15, `stops-name` from 17, with the places' muting expression. `set_curbside` / `set_stops` send a source only when its data changed.
- Every feature carries `hot: "s-<uid>"` and both sources use `promoteId: "hot"`: a street's features share one id and feature state (`hot`, `editable`). MapLibre keys state by source and id, so duplicates are fine (the e2e pins `editable === false` on a muted street's use).
- Layers go before `places-junction` (over streets, under junction marks, zone ticks, draft) so what is pressed is what is seen (on a junction's ground the junction is pressed); `showPlaces` moves them under the marks when places arrive later (the e2e reads `getStyle().layers`). `web/mapcanvas.js` only adds the layer ids to `hotAt` and the sources to the feature-state loop.
- Row and screen-reader label count what the street draws (`map-curb`, `map-stops`, `curb_count` / `stop_count`). Nothing is saved differently.
- `querySourceFeatures` is empty below the layers' `minzoom`; the e2e reads `getSource(..).serialize().data`.

## Network checks (`map/model/network.rs`, `map/view/network_view.rs`, `city/model/network_read.rs`; #63, #69)
- Findings spanning the loaded streets. At the city's level of the details (`#network-panel`, `#network-lead`, `#network-slot`) each check is a row with its count (`NetworkChecks`, `#net-go-<id>`); a row shows the check at a level of its own (`MapLevel::Check`, `MapVm::show_check` / `shown_check`, `#map-check`, `NetworkCheckPane`): its count, what the network checks are (`net-note`), its source and its findings. A place a finding names is a button (`.net-link`, `data-hot`) that shows it in the details, from where Open opens it; a press on nothing on the map keeps the check.
- `City::network_read(region)` makes a plain `NetworkRead` (`StreetRead`, `EndRead` from the zone meeting the node via `Street::at_end`, `NodeRead` with a junction's `ArmRead`s as the junction editor reads them; a pedestrian arm has no crossing). Sidewalk runs use `street::model::walkway_access` / `ACCESS_LANE_MM`.
- `map::model::network::check` is the pure rule engine returning `NetworkReport { findings: Vec<NetworkFinding { rule: Check, severity: Severity, said: Said, places }>, unreadable }`. `unreadable` counts junctions the editor could not read (`NodeRead.unreadable`) or with an arm whose street is not in the network; the lead says so (`net-unreadable`), and such a junction is never taken for one with nothing found.
- Six checks, every figure a placeholder, UNVERIFIED:
  - **cycling**: bike-lane streets join at a node only where both have one at that end; each group but the biggest is a link cut off (`net-cycling-island`, Problem). A bike lane stopping where other streets meet and none has one: `net-cycling-ends`, Notice.
  - **access**: pedestrian street without an unbroken sidewalk run of `ACCESS_LANE_MM` in some zone, Notice. Absolute (not against the first layout): an imported street never wide enough keeps its notice until changed.
  - **crossings**: an arm taking traffic, neither pedestrian nor freeway, with no crossing (Problem) or one wider than `MAX_CROSSING_MM` (4.5 m, Notice).
  - **lanes**: two streets meeting alone at a plain connection, neither pedestrian nor freeway, whose driving+transit lanes differ by more than `MAX_LANE_STEP` (1).
  - **transit**: a transit lane stopping where others meet and none has one, Notice.
  - **walking** (`Check::Walking`, `net-rule-walking`):
    - `net-walking-ends`, Notice: sidewalk stops where another non-freeway street goes on without one.
    - `net-walking-block`, Notice: non-freeway block over `MAX_BLOCK_MM` (250 m; the hand-made city's 240 m pass); names its street and end junctions.
    - `net-walking-island`, Problem: walkable streets join at a plain connection, and at a junction only through crossable arms (pedestrian street, or arm with a crossing; an unreadable junction or arm of no known street gets the benefit of the doubt). Every route but the biggest is an island; its places are its streets, the junctions where it meets an uncrossable arm or unwalkable street, and its non-freeway streets without a sidewalk. A route touching nothing that cuts it is a fragment of the area's box: not reported.
- Curb ramps are not modelled (covered only as sidewalk continuity and crossings).
- `MapVm::network_rules` / `network_lead` / `network_count` (`net-*` in `map/i18n`; `NetworkCheck` per rule, `NetworkItem`s linking `street_href` / `junction_href`) rerun only when the city view changes and touch neither `status`, `checks_lead` nor Area rules counts.
- Caps: `NETWORK_SHOWN` (50) findings per check (count is all; `net-more-findings`), `NETWORK_PLACES` (8) places per finding (`net-more-places`). A city with no places shows only the lead.
- Known limits: the bike graph joins streets at nodes, not lanes, and does not follow a bike lane through a pedestrian street; time-of-day variants ignored; a place a finding names is shown in the details, but the map is not centred on it.

## Design review report (#68 PRs 1 to 3; `cityloom_report/`, `map/vm/report.rs`, `map/vm/report_review.rs`, `shell/share_view.rs`, `web/report.js`, `e2e/tests/report.spec.ts`)
- Share menu (`#share-btn`, `#share-menu`, `#report`, `ui-report`) downloads a Letter PDF `cityloom-<area slug>.pdf`: cover + summary of the city's checks (two pages), then one page per changed place, then the review pages, the Sources pages (the rules that cite a source, as many pages as they need, #78; see `shared/CLAUDE.md`) and the appendix. The editors' menu keeps `#print`, no `#report` (`ShareMenu report=false`).
- `cityloom_report` is data only (`ReportData`, `Block`, serde; the editor depends on it). `cityloom_report_pdf` is the writer, its own crate and wasm module (`web/pkg/cityloom_report_pdf*`; krilla, tagged PDF/UA-1, "?" for a character Noto Sans lacks), loaded on first use by `web/report.js` (`cityloomReportPdf`; fetches `web/fonts/NotoSans-{Regular,Bold}.ttf`; retries after a failure).
- **Nothing in the editor may depend on `cityloom_report_pdf`** (feature unification would put krilla in the editor's wasm: 7.0 MB instead of 4.8 MB). `scripts/build.sh` fails if `web/pkg/cityloom_editor.js` contains `report_pdf`; `cargo tree -p cityloom_editor -e normal | grep -c krilla` must be 0 (the editor's tests dev-depend on the writer).
- Ports `PdfWriter`, `Downloads` (`save` returns a `Result`); fakes in `shared/ports.rs`. `MapVm::download_report` builds the data from the city view (`map/vm/report.rs`, pure). A failure says `map-report-failed` via the `Announcer` and shows `ReportNotice` in `#fit-slot` (`MapVm::report_failed` / `dismiss_report_notice`; `#report-notice` `role="status"`, `#report-notice-close`, `map-report-dismiss`; reuses `fit bad` tokens; goes on the next attempt or on close).
- Wording in `map/i18n` (`map-report-*`), so the file follows the page language. The e2e reads the file with pdf.js (`pdfjs-dist`).
- **Place pages** (`map/vm/report.rs`, `place_pages_up_to`): one `Block::Figures` group per changed place after the summary (junctions, then streets, in the Changes list's order; at most `MAX_PLACES` = 40, with a line saying how many are left out). Each has a heading, what changed (width / control, before and after), Before and After figures with alt text, for a street the width-by-mode table before and after, and a Check / Before / After / Detail table of the checks whose result differs (at most `MAX_FAILING_ROWS` = 8; an unchanged or both-passing place says `All N checks pass`). Before is the layout as imported from OpenStreetMap: `City::street_editor_today` / `junction_editor_today` give the editor of the imported state (derived, never saved).
- `Block::Figures` is data in `cityloom_report`; `cityloom_report_pdf` draws it as tagged Figures with alt text. A page that overflows continues on the next; a line that cannot fit makes the writer fail rather than cut it. Figures are flat vector drawings of the 2.5D: `shared::flat` (pure; its colour table mirrors the CSS tokens, and `every_flat_colour_matches_the_css` fails on drift), `street::view::drawing::figure`, `junction::view::figure::figure`. They omit what a flat vector cannot say (textures, joints, `Elem` marks, shelter opacity, outline casings), and a note under the figure says so.
- **Review pages** (`map/vm/report_review.rs`, pure; built in `MapVm::report_data` from the network report and the areas' streets, both read untracked; only when the city has places): one page with the network effects (a Pass / Notice / Fail row for each of the six checks, then for each check that found something a table of its findings, at most `MAX_ROWS` = 10, each naming at most `MAX_PLACE_NAMES` = 2 places) and the area rules (a row for each declared area with its streets inside and in conflict, then the streets that break their rule, at most `MAX_AREA_STREETS` = 20; a notice that none are declared); then the appendix (the status of the thresholds, a glossary of Pass, Fail, Problem, Notice, Before and After, network check, area rule, placeholder, and the credits). Wording is `map-report-*` (the findings reuse `net-*`). A page that outgrows a sheet continues on the next (the writer), so a worst case of every check full is tested through the real writer in both languages. Sources of the rules are #78's (`shared::sources`), not repeated here.
- Known limits: whole city only (no area/place scope), no map of the proposal yet (the issue's proposal map is not in any of the three PRs), the junction figure is small and its street labels can clip at the figure's edge, nothing in it is verified engineering.
